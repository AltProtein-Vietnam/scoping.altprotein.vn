### New calculations and comparisons (ch21)

All are marked "(our calculation)" or "(our comparison)" in the text.

1. **Unit conversion.** USD 1,000 per t of protein = USD 1 per kg of protein; 1 t CO2e per t = 1 kg CO2e per kg. Stated in the method note and in 21.5.
2. **Fungal floor against food.** Floor USD 1,870 per t of protein (F3.3.2) = USD 1.87 per kg, inside the ch16 commodity plant-protein band of USD 1.7 to 2.4 per kg of protein (2025 border prices).
3. **Fungal planning range against food.** Planning range (F3.3.2) 2030: USD 3,000 to 12,500 per t; 2040: 2,400 to 10,800; 2050: 2,200 to 9,500. As USD per kg: 3.0 to 12.5; 2.4 to 10.8; 2.2 to 9.5. Against commodity plant proteins (1.7 to 2.4) and egg and dairy proteins (7 to 11): the low end is below egg and dairy from 2030, at the top of the commodity band in 2040 and inside it in 2050; the high end is above egg and dairy in 2030 and inside their range in 2040 and 2050.
4. **Protein from power against food.** Vietnamese estimate (F3.2.4) USD 6.9 to 12.6 per kg (2030), 4.7 to 8.6 (2040), 3.3 to 6.5 (2050). The 2030 range spans egg and dairy (7 to 11); the 2050 range is below egg and dairy and above commodity plant proteins (1.7 to 2.4).
5. **Food share of the S-ALT 2050 glucose need.** Inputs from `data/balance_outputs.csv` and `data/balance_assumptions.csv`: microbial feed protein 514.1 kt (BLO-1121), gas-route share 0.5 (BLA-210), food protein 144.6 kt (BLO-1125), fermented food share 0.5 (BLA-214), 4.0 t glucose per t of protein (BLA-215), total glucose 1,317.5 kt (BLO-1128). Sugar-route feed protein 514.1 x 0.5 = 257 kt; fermented food protein 144.6 x 0.5 = 72.3 kt; food glucose 72.3 x 4.0 = 289 kt, about 0.29 Mt, 22% of 1,317.5 kt (feed 78%). Matches the model formula `glucose = (mp_sugar_feed + food_ferm) x glucose_per_protein` in `tools/balance_model.py`.
6. **Carbon against food.** Cassava-based microbial protein 3.8 to 11.7 t CO2e per t of protein (existing ch21 figure) against D8 factors: pig meat 76.1 and poultry 57.0 kg CO2e per kg of protein (Poore and Nemecek global means, the package's upper bound, DG-274); textured soy 8.36 (1.31 to 19.75). Reading: far below meat, about the same as plant protein.
