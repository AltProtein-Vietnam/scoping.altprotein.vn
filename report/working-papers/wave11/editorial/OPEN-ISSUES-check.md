- **Source wording.** Under the heading "Productivity trends": "Global production of agricultural and fish commodities is projected to expand by about 14 percent through to 2034, mainly enabled by productivity gains in middle-income countries. - **Confidence.** High.
- **Recommendation.** Keep. Optional, to state the horizon in the sentence as the `{fx:projection}` rule asks: "World output of meat, dairy and eggs rises 17% by 2034, while global herds and flocks grow 7% [@PRB-04] {general|High} {fx:projection}." ### C2. The heme gloss (lines 69, 81, 205)

- **Line 69 (table, precision fermentation row).** Current: "US regulators raised no questions on Impossible's heme, a protein it uses for flavour (2018; the burger has sold since 2016), and Perfect Day's whey (2020)". Cited: PRA-10, PRA-07, PRA-11, PRA-14, PRA-12, SCI-01. The relevant source is PRA-11.
- **PRA-11, read in full (US FDA, response letter on GRAS Notice GRN 000737, 23 July 2018, https://www.fda.gov/media/116243/download, PDF text extracted, accessed 2026-10-01):**
  - "The subject of the notice is soy leghemoglobin preparation from a strain of Pichia pastoris (soy leghemoglobin preparation) for use at a level up to 0.8% soybean leghemoglobin protein to optimize flavor in ground beef analogue products intended to be cooked."
