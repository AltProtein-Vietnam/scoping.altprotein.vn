# Alternative seafood across platforms: GFI solutions screened for Vietnam

Line: L4 alternative seafood across platforms (wave 13). Source prefix: SLS. Date: 15 October 2026.

GFI is the Good Food Institute. Its Solutions Database is cited as GSD-01 and each entry by its GFI number (GFI-005 and so on). Tags follow PROTOCOL section 4: `[evidence label, confidence, source IDs]`, plus foresight or demand type where relevant.

## Verdict

Vietnam's seafood industry is a real base for one near-term alternative-seafood route, and it is not cultivated seafood. Vietnam has a surimi and fish-cake export trade of more than USD 344 million (2025) run by more than 100 exporters. Its wild-fish raw material is squeezed by a planned 27% cut in capture by 2030, the trawler cap and the EU "yellow card". Lab science on fish-plant protein blends is mature. That supports one candidate play: **hybrid fish cakes and fish balls**, with 10 to 30% of the fish protein replaced by plant protein (SLS-C1), first for the domestic *chả cá* (fish cake) trade and finished-product exports. Its weak point is demand: no buyer asks for it, and Korea's largest fish-cake maker is moving to more fish, not less. The line also **changes T8**. Food-relevant muscle cell lines now exist for grouper and Asian seabass, two species farmed in Vietnam, while catfish lines are virology tools, shrimp has no true line, and pangasius sells at about USD 1.02 to 1.15 per kg at the farm gate. Cultivated and fermentation-made surimi are not for Vietnam: Vietnamese tropical surimi sells for USD 1.67 to 2.24 per kg. Yield class: **added a candidate or route**; secondarily **changed a conclusion** (a proposed T8 species change, resting on metadata-level evidence for the new cell lines). The omega-3, scaffold, proliferation, LCA and showcase entries are clean "monitor" or "not now" results.

## Headline findings

1. **Surimi is a USD 300 to 420 million a year Vietnamese export.** Surimi and fish-cake exports were more than USD 344 million in 2025, up 15% on 2024 but below the 2022 peak. Korea took nearly USD 83 million, China more than USD 54 million and the EU more than USD 35 million (+99%). The top five markets took more than 81% [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-01, revealed]. Over five years the trade earned USD 300 to 420 million a year, 4 to 5% of seafood exports, through more than 100 exporters led by DALU SURIMI, CASES and KICOIMEX [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-03].
2. **Vietnamese tropical surimi is cheap.** Partner-reported imports of frozen fish meat (HS 030499, mostly surimi in these flows) from Vietnam in 2025 were 49.3 kt at USD 1.67 per kg in Korea, 35.4 kt at USD 2.24 per kg in Thailand and 26.3 kt at USD 2.20 per kg in China. Together that is 111.0 kt worth USD 219.6 million [VN-direct, High, SLS-05, revealed]. Korea's figure (USD 82.2 million) matches VASEP's Korea surimi and fish-cake figure (nearly USD 83 million), which supports reading these flows as surimi (our inference).
3. **The raw material is under policy pressure.** Capture is planned to fall from 3.83 Mt (2025) to 2.8 Mt (2030), and Decision 389 caps trawlers at about 8,360 boats by 2030 [VN-direct, High, MAC-15; NTS-08; VN-direct, Medium, AQF-15, projection 2030]. Exporters already cite "domestic raw material shortages" and difficulty importing fish under IUU (illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing) rules [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-04]. The EU yellow card issued in October 2017 was still in place in March 2026 [VN-direct, Low, SLS-22]. This is the Vietnam-specific reason to stretch fish protein.
4. **Plant protein is 3.6 to 7.5 times cheaper per kg of protein than tropical surimi** (our calculation, inputs under "Calculation inputs"). Chinese soy isolate lands at USD 1.96 to 2.44 per kg of protein [VN-direct, Low, BUY-07]; surimi at USD 1.67 to 2.24 per kg costs about USD 8.8 to 14.8 per kg of protein on an assumed 20 to 25% dry matter [VN-direct, Low, SLS-05; SLS-20]. Replacing 20% of the fish protein would cut protein cost by about 14 to 17% (our calculation) [VN-direct, Low, SLS-05; SLS-20; BUY-07, estimate 2025].
5. **The blend science is mature; the tolerance is 30 to 40%.** We found 111 papers since 2018 on plant proteins in surimi gels [general, Medium, SLS-08]. In one sensory study, panels rejected surimi gels at 30% replacement with wheat or rice protein and 40% with soy or pea; gel strength and whiteness fell as replacement rose [general, Medium, SLS-07, tested]. Vietnamese fish-cake research already adds starch (10%) and fat (10%) [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-25]; we found no Vietnamese study of fish-plant protein substitution [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-06].
6. **Plant "fish cakes" already sell in Vietnam, beside surimi ones.** An Nhien's *chả cá chay* (plant fish cake, 19.7 g protein per 100 g, wheat-based) is VND 130,000 per kg; a P.G.P version is VND 151,800 per kg; surimi "fish tofu" from LC Foods and Viet Sin sells at about VND 171,000 per kg on a business-to-business platform [VN-direct, Medium, FORM-01; ECO-40; ECR-06, revealed]. LC Foods is also a named T1 buyer.
7. **The demand signal from the largest market runs the other way.** Dongwon F&B opened a 40 t a day fish-cake and crab-stick plant in Korea in June 2026 and is focusing on premium products with 80% or more fish meat [general, Medium, SLS-11, revealed]. Plant-based seafood sales are falling in the US and Germany [general, High, EXP-02; EXP-05, revealed].
8. **A plant fish cake has about half the carbon footprint of a surimi one in a neighbouring market.** A Singapore LCA (life cycle assessment) found 8.94 kg CO2 eq per kg for a surimi fish cake (surimi 76.1% of it) against 4.11 to 5.36 for a brown rice protein fish cake [VN-adjacent, Medium, SLS-12]. Rice protein links this to T10.
9. **For cultivated seafood, the species should change.** Food-oriented muscle cell lines have been published for seven-band grouper (Korea, 2024) and Asian seabass (India, 2025, with lab-grown "fish meat" and micro-tissue) [general, Low, SLS-14; SLS-15; SLS-16]. Striped catfish has six cell-line papers by title, all for virology or toxicology; we found no food-relevant line [VN-adjacent, Medium, SLS-13; SCI-10]. Shrimp has no true continuous line [general, High, SCI-12].
10. **Pangasius cannot carry cultivated costs.** Modelled cultivated cost (USD 37 to 51 per kg of wet cells) is 32 to 50 times the pangasius farm-gate price and 8 to 13 times the retail fillet price (our calculation) [general, High, COST-47; VN-direct, Medium, FM-11; DIE-15; DIE-19].
11. **Vietnam has algal DHA strains but no omega-3 industry.** The VAST Institute of Biotechnology holds *Schizochytrium mangrovei* PQ6 (105.25 g/L biomass and 43.40% lipid in a 30 L fed-batch run) and other thraustochytrid strains [VN-direct, High, SLS-17; SLS-18; SLS-19]. We found no commercial production. For alternative seafood Vietnam would buy omega-3 oil.
12. **Cultivated and fermentation-made surimi are not for Vietnam.** GFI cites a best-case cultivated cost of USD 6.43 per kg against surimi at USD 3 to 6 per kg [general, Low, GSD-01]. Vietnamese surimi sells for USD 1.67 to 2.24 per kg (finding 2), so the gap is wider here. We found no approved cultivated surimi or fish ball anywhere; no cultivated seafood is approved for human food in Asia [general, Medium, NOV-25; RGN-04].

#### 1.2 Raw-material and waste constraints

- **Policy squeeze on wild fish.** Capture fell to 3.83 Mt in 2025 and is planned to reach 2.8 Mt in 2030; trawlers are capped at about 8,360 [VN-direct, High, MAC-15; NTS-08; VN-direct, Medium, AQF-15, projection 2030]. Surimi depends on trawl-caught low-value marine fish (our inference from SLS-03 and AQF-05).
- **Shortages already reported.** In 2024 exporters cited "domestic raw material shortages and challenges in importing raw materials due to IUU regulations", and a global surplus of pollock surimi pressing on tropical surimi [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-04]. In 2023 the main problems named were fish preservation, wastewater treatment and skilled labour [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-03].
- **Waste.** GFI cites 60 to 70% of the whole fish discarded before surimi processing and a further 10 to 45% of protein lost in washing [general, Low, GSD-01]. Surimi keeps the myofibrillar proteins; sarcoplasmic proteins leave with the wash water [VN-direct, Medium, RD-52].
- **An asset that does not need trawling.** Pangasius processors already make commercial surimi from filleting side streams: a Nha Trang University study compared fish protein isolates with "commercial surimi produced at the company from the same batch of rest materials", and recovered 83.0 to 88.9% of side-stream protein by pH shift [VN-direct, Medium, RD-52]. Can Tho University made surimi gels of 338 to 377 g.cm from pangasius trimmings [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-10]. Pangasius by-products are 62 to 67% of the fish and 1,938.8 kt of pangasius was farmed in 2025 [VN-direct, Low, MAC-45; VN-direct, High, MAC-15]. This links to P5, the by-product atlas.

Commercial blends exist in a weak sense: Vietnamese fish-cake formulations already use about 10% starch and 10% fat [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-25], and a Can Tho study (search snippet, not read) used 0.25% soy protein with 0.25% gluten as a binder. We found no commercial fish cake that replaces a stated share of fish protein with plant protein, in Vietnam or abroad.

#### 1.4 Rules and standards for blends

- **Domestic market.** A fish-plant fish cake is an ordinary processed food, self-declared under Decree 15/2018/ND-CP [VN-direct, High, REG-02]. If the name uses the fish (*chả cá*), the fish share must be printed: Decree 43/2017 Art 13(4), carried into Decree 37/2026 [VN-direct, Medium, LBL-06; LBL-08]. The name must not mislead about composition (Art 11) [VN-direct, Medium, LBL-06].
- **Surimi standard.** TCVN 8682:2025 (ISO 23855:2021) on frozen surimi replaced the 2011 version and is voluntary. On the snippet we could see, surimi is washed, dewatered fish meat with optional cryoprotectants, graded by gel strength. A plant blend would therefore be sold as a fish-paste product, not as surimi [VN-direct, Low, SLS-21]. We could not read the text (HTTP 403).
- **Export markets.** We did not check Korean, Japanese or EU rules on fish content of fish cakes, or how the EU treats a fish-plant composite product at the border. This is an open question (OQ candidate 3).

#### 1.5 Would a hybrid line be a candidate play?

Yes, but as a finished-product play, not a "hybrid surimi" raw material. Frozen surimi is bought by gel-strength grade and formulated by the buyer, so Vietnamese exporters cannot sell a blend into that trade (our inference from SLS-02 and SLS-21). The candidate is hybrid fish cakes, fish balls and fish tofu for the domestic market, canteens and finished-product exports. It is scored in `data_candidates.csv` (SLS-C1) and in section 4.

### 3. Fish cell lines, proliferation, scaffolds and omega-3 uptake (GFI-110, GFI-097, GFI-071, GFI-072, GFI-088)

- **Existing lines for Vietnamese species.** Catfish lines are in India and Taiwan and serve virology [VN-adjacent, Medium, SCI-10; SCI-11; SCI-46; SCI-47; SLS-13]. Two gill-line papers (2021 and 2022) appear by title; the line name asked in OQ-100 is still not confirmed [VN-adjacent, Low, SLS-13]. Snakehead lines (18 title matches) and grouper lines (185) are mostly from India, China and Taiwan, for virus work [general, Medium, SLS-13].
- **Food-relevant lines since GFI wrote (2024).** Seven-band grouper immortalised muscle line, Pukyong National University, 2024; Asian seabass muscle and fin lines used to make small lab-grown fish meat and micro-tissue on scaffolds, Thiruvalluvar University, 2025; Asian seabass primary myoblasts (Australia, 2026) and a barramundi muscle-line study (James Cook University, 2026) [general, Low, SLS-14; SLS-15; SLS-16; SLS-13]. All metadata only.
- **Vietnam.** We found no fish or shrimp cell line developed in Vietnam, consistent with the study [VN-direct, Medium, RD-01; BIB-01]. Vietnamese strengths are adjacent: growth-factor expression in *Pichia* at VNU-HCM [VN-direct, Medium, RD-36], surimi and fish-protein science at Nha Trang University and Can Tho University [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-06; RD-52; SLS-10].
- **Partners that would matter.** Thiruvalluvar University (seabass lines), Pukyong National University (grouper line), James Cook University Singapore (barramundi), ICAR-NBFGR (catfish and snakehead lines). Nha Trang University and RIA3 (Research Institute for Aquaculture No. 3, Nha Trang) are the natural Vietnamese hosts for marine species (our inference; not contacted).
- **Proliferation and temperature.** GFI's table gives 42 hours doubling for an Asian seabass embryonic-stem-like line at 28 C, against several days for many fish lines [general, Low, GSD-01]. Warm-water lines are a reason to prefer tropical marine species.
- **Scaffolds and omega-3 uptake.** No Vietnam-specific angle. Vietnam would buy the results.

### 4. Omega-3 for human food (GFI-073, GFI-074, GFI-075)

- **Vietnamese asset.** The VAST Institute of Biotechnology has isolated DHA-producing thraustochytrids: *S. mangrovei* PQ6 from Phu Quoc in An Giang (former Kien Giang), with 105.25 g/L biomass and 43.40% lipid in a 30 L fed-batch run [VN-direct, High, SLS-17], and *Aurantiochytrium* SC145 with 40.73 to 44.00% omega 3-6-9 fatty acids and no oral toxicity in mice over 90 days [VN-direct, High, SLS-18]. VNU Hanoi has ten strains from Xuan Thuy mangrove in Ninh Binh (former Nam Dinh) [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-19].
- **No industry.** We found no commercial algal omega-3 production in Vietnam (searches of OpenAlex only; SLS-19). Pangasius oil carries only 0.17 to 0.31% EPA plus DHA, so domestic fish oil is no source either [VN-adjacent, Medium, AQF-29].
- **Who needs it.** Surimi and fish cakes are low in fat, so SLS-C1 does not need omega-3 (GFI makes the same point for surimi) [general, Low, GSD-01]. Feed omega-3 is context only (RM-07).
- **Reading.** Vietnam would buy algal oil for any alternative seafood. The VAST strains are a research asset for L2's strain-library question and a possible later tolling customer for T5; not a play.
- **Oxidation (GFI-075).** Hot climate and weak cold chains make oxidation a real issue for any omega-3-enriched product, but this is generic food science Vietnam would buy (our judgement).

### 5. LCA, consumer and sensory research, showcase (GFI-093, GFI-105, GFI-032)

- **LCA.** GFI said in 2022 that no LCA compared alternative and conventional seafood. One now exists for the fish-cake format: Singapore, 2026 (finding 8) [VN-adjacent, Medium, SLS-12]. Vietnamese conventional LCAs exist for intensive pangasius (2011) and Asian aquaculture products including Vietnam (2015) [VN-direct, Medium, SLS-24]. Our query found only six LCA records on alternative seafood [general, Medium, SLS-24]. Vietnam does not need its own alternative-seafood LCA until it has a product; SLS-C1 can reuse SLS-12's method.
- **Consumer and sensory.** Part III covers consumers in general, and ch14 notes that *thịt nhân tạo* (artificial meat) is a loaded name [VN-direct, High, NOV-69]. We found no Vietnamese sensory study of fish analogues. The useful test is narrow: a sensory panel and a buyer trial of hybrid *chả cá* at 10 to 30% substitution, built into SLS-C1's kill tests.
- **Showcase ("Rainbow roll 2.0").** Raw-fish sushi made from cultivated cells has no route in Vietnam, no domestic demand before the mid-2030s (TPP-10) and depends on approvals abroad. Not for Vietnam now.

## Calculation inputs (our calculation)

From `calc_l4.py` (output in `calc_l4_output.txt`):

- Surimi unit value: USD 1.67 (Korea) to 2.24 (Thailand) per kg, 2025 [SLS-05].
- Protein in surimi dry matter: 72% protein at about 5% moisture in threadfin bream surimi powder gives 75.8% of dry matter [SLS-20].
- **Assumption (not sourced):** frozen surimi has 20 to 25% dry matter, so 15.2 to 18.9% protein. Low.
- Surimi protein cost: USD 8.8 to 14.8 per kg of protein. Soy isolate: USD 1.96 to 2.44 per kg of protein [BUY-07]. Ratio 3.6 to 7.5.
- Saving on protein cost: 7 to 9% at 10% substitution, 14 to 17% at 20%, 22 to 26% at 30%. This ignores extra binders, process changes, any price discount for a lower fish share, and the loss of gel strength.
- Pangasius: VND 26,500 to 30,000 per kg farm gate (FM-11) and VND 100,000 to 116,800 per kg retail fillet (DPP-008, DPP-009), converted at 26,000 VND per USD.

## 4. Candidate play SLS-C1: hybrid fish cakes and fish balls with plant protein

- **What.** Vietnamese fish-cake, fish-ball and fish-tofu makers replace 10 to 30% of the fish protein with plant protein (soy isolate now; mung bean or rice protein from T10 later), sold with the fish share on the label. First in the domestic market and canteens, then finished-product exports (Japan and the US buy most Vietnamese fish cakes).
- **Why Vietnam.** A USD 344 million surimi and fish-cake trade with more than 100 exporters; wild-fish raw material squeezed by the 2030 capture cut, the trawler cap and IUU rules; pangasius side-stream surimi; plant *chả cá chay* already on shelves; surimi gel science at Nha Trang University and Can Tho University.
- **What has to be true.** Buyers accept a declared fish share below today's products; sensory panels accept 10 to 30% substitution in Vietnamese formats; plant protein stays well below fish protein per kg of protein; no export rule blocks fish-plant composites.
- **Kill tests.** Sensory panel or buyer rejects at 15% substitution; no maker agrees to a trial run within a year; the per-kg-protein price gap falls below about 2 times; target export buyers set a minimum fish content above the hybrid level (Korea's premium trend is 80% fish or more).
- **First customers.** Domestic fish-cake makers (LC Foods, Viet Sin); surimi exporters with finished-product lines (DALU SURIMI, CASES and KICOIMEX are the leading exporters; their finished-product lines are unverified); caterers whose menus carry *chả cá* dishes (Phu My Gold sample menus) [VN-direct, Medium, UPL-30, stated].
- **Partners.** Nha Trang University and Can Tho University (surimi gels); soy isolate suppliers; T10 developers; VASEP.
- **Scores (calibrated against T1 and T10).** Market pull 2, Vietnam advantage 3, technical readiness 4, route to market 5, capital efficiency 5, defensibility 2, public value 3. Balanced score 3.50 (our calculation with the balanced preset); it ranks third or fourth of eight under every preset, behind T1 and T4 and sometimes T5 (our calculation with `play_weight_presets.csv`). The score is driven by ease (route and capital), not by demand. The binding test is a buyer trial; if market pull is really 1, the balanced score falls to 3.35.
- **Why it earns a place.** It is the only near-term route that uses Vietnam's seafood processing industry for human food; T8 is 5 to 10 years or more out. It beats T1 on capital efficiency (5 against 4). The lead may prefer to fold it into TPP-03 (hybrid extender) as a fish-paste segment, with T10 as a supplier; we record both options.

## Next-wave candidates

1. **Hybrid fish-cake trial (deeper, field).** Phone LC Foods, Viet Sin and one leading surimi exporter; ask whether they use soy isolate now and whether they would run a 10 to 20% substitution trial with a sensory panel at Nha Trang University. Desk research cannot close it. A yes moves SLS-C1 market pull from 2 to 3; a no kills it.
2. **Domestic fish-paste market size (different kind).** No source gives domestic output of *chả cá*, fish balls and fish tofu. VASEP's surimi committee or the NSO industrial output series may. This decides whether SLS-C1 is a domestic or an export play.
3. **Export rules for fish-plant composites (deeper, desk).** Korean Food Code fish-cake definitions, Japanese labelling of *kamaboko*, and EU composite-product border rules. Desk research could close it in a few hours.
4. **Grouper and seabass in Vietnam (different kind).** Farm output and farm-gate prices from Khanh Hoa provincial data or RIA3. Needed before T8 names species.
5. Further desk work on omega-3, scaffolds, proliferation or LCA would be noise for this study.
