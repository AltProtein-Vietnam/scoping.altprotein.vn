# Coefficient Giving: Alternatives to Animal Products, notes

Prepared for AltProtein Vietnam, "Alternative protein in Vietnam: a supply-side scoping study". Pages read on 15 October 2026.

## 1. Strategy, as the site states it

The Farm Animal Welfare Fund page lists alternatives to animal products as one of four strategy areas, funding work to speed up plant-based and cultivated products that reduce demand for factory-farmed meat, dairy and eggs. The grants tab for the focus area says the goal is to make alt-protein products more appealing, available and affordable, and names three groups it supports: researchers working on better and cheaper products, advocates pushing food companies to sell more of them, and policy experts arguing for more public R&D funding.

The focus area page (https://coefficientgiving.org/funds/farm-animal-welfare/alternatives-to-animal-products/) says the fund aims to accelerate innovation and adoption "particularly in areas that are neglected by the mainstream alt-protein market". It credits advocates with securing over USD 2 billion in public funding for alternative protein research and infrastructure, and with new research centres in the US and Israel. It names its current priorities as engaging industry and governments on R&D priorities, building academic interest in key technology bottlenecks, and countering anti-competitive policies from incumbents.

Note a mismatch: the "Grantee Wins" box on the fund page says nearly USD 1 billion in public funding, while the focus area page says over USD 2 billion.

## 2. Calls for proposals

**Request for Proposals: Alternative Protein R&D (Farm Animal Welfare Fund), 2026.** This is the taste research call.

- **Amount:** up to USD 10 million, with the option to go beyond that for strong proposals (Vegconomist, 9 June 2026). A Coefficient Giving staff post on the EA Forum (Abhi Kumar, Associate Program Officer) was later edited to say the call grew to USD 30 million, with a second round for larger projects.
- **Deadlines:** round 1 open until 10 August 2026, with rolling awards and final decisions by 30 November 2026. Round 2 open until 15 September 2026 (EA Forum edit).
- **Award size:** a background check of the official page reported awards of about USD 100,000 to 1 million over 2 to 3 years, open globally to universities, institutes, companies and public bodies. I could not confirm this myself (see limits).
- **Priorities:**
  1. reducing off-flavours in plant and fermentation-derived proteins;
  2. fat systems and substitutes for animal fat in flavour generation;
  3. reducing or replacing egg (bakery, dressings, pasta);
  4. mapping the flavour of fish so it can be replicated.
- **Fish species:** the same background check reported that the fish priority names tilapia, milkfish, carp, pond loach and catfish. These are species farmed at scale in Vietnam, so worth confirming.
- **Awardees:** none announced on any page opened.
- **Official page:** https://coefficientgiving.org/funds/farm-animal-welfare/request-for-proposals-alternative-protein-rd/ now returns "Page not found" on the live site.
- **Sources:** https://vegconomist.com/investments-finance/coefficient-giving-opens-10m-funding-call-alternative-protein-taste-research/ and https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/bDqdfMXe6YuYnwMDA/good-tasting-alt-meat-is-a-scientific-problem-we-can-solve

The site's "Apply for Funding" page lists no open call for farm animal welfare or alternative proteins as of 15 October 2026.

  - field-building: 10

**Grants with a verified or probable published output:** 4 (CG-G013, CG-G019, CG-G020, CG-G044).

**Two further outputs** were commissioned by Open Philanthropy or Coefficient Giving but are not among the 61 listed grants:
- the Humbird cultured meat techno-economic analysis (CG-O001);
- a Rethink Priorities egg replacement report (CG-O007).

## 4. Vietnam and Southeast Asia

- No grant in the list names Vietnam or Southeast Asia, and a site search for "Vietnam" returns nothing.
- Asia-linked grants:
  - CG-G020, Food Frontier, "Promoting Plant-Based Meat Alternatives in Asia" (USD 500,000, August 2022);
  - CG-G037, The Good Food Institute Asia Pacific, nutrition gaps research (USD 22,500, July 2024).
- **Food Frontier's Asia report (CG-O005):** it assessed 11 Asian markets and ranked China, Singapore, South Korea, Japan and Thailand highest. A background check found trade press (FoodNavigator, August 2023) reporting Vietnam among the least favourable markets for plant-based growth in that study. Food Frontier's own pages checked do not mention Vietnam, so read the full report before citing this.
- **Site mention of Southeast Asia:** the only one is in "Our Progress in 2024 and Plans for 2025" (March 2025), and it concerns cage-free and broiler work by Open Wing Alliance groups, not alternative proteins.
- **Most relevant opening for Vietnam:** the 2026 taste RFP's fish flavour priority (see section 2), if the species list is confirmed.

## 5. Outputs most relevant to protein manufacturing

1. **CG-O001, Humbird (2021).** The reference cost model for cultivated meat. Fed-batch production costs USD 37 per kg of wet cell mass and perfusion USD 51 per kg, at plants of about 6.8 to 6.9 kilotonnes a year. Total capital investment is USD 328 million and USD 663 million respectively.
2. **CG-O004, Purdue zein work under the FFAR grant (probable).** Covers plant protein texturisation. FFAR also reports a high-protein chickpea (NC-1) from the NuCicer project. These FFAR figures come from a background check of FFAR pages, which I have not opened myself:
   - the project has Open Philanthropy as a matching funder;
   - the chickpea gave at least a 75% protein increase over commercial varieties;
   - over 10,000 acres were planted in 2026.
3. **CG-O007, Rethink Priorities (2025).** Covers egg replacement in processed foods. It cites an IMPROVE (2023) study that tested 10 commercial protein ingredients and found potato protein closest to egg white. That study may be the output of CG-G023.

**Nothing found on fermentation or single-cell protein.** No verified output from these grants covers fermentation cost or capacity, or single-cell or microbial protein from sugar, starch, cassava, natural gas or CO2. A background check reported a gas-fermentation techno-economic analysis funded through Synonym/Roebling (announced November 2023), but found no published output.

## 6. Limits

- **No grant pages.** The site has no CSV or spreadsheet download, and grant titles in the table are not links.
  - Old Open Philanthropy grant URLs (for example openphilanthropy.org/?p=1330 and ?p=1379) now redirect to "Page not found" on coefficientgiving.org.
  - So `purpose_summary` is blank for every grant, and `grant_url` points to the focus-area grants table.
  - A background check quoted purpose text from those old grant pages. Its tool may have served a cached copy, so I did not use that text. Examples:
    - FFAR chickpea and corn zein (NuCicer and Purdue);
    - FFAR "Farm Animal Welfare Research" co-funding a plant protein RFP for plant-based meat;
    - IMPROVE contract of EUR 60,500;
    - Food Frontier USD 200,000 over two years for the Asia-Pacific (this matches the June 2020 grant).
- **Geography is not shown on the site.** Values come only from a place named in the grant title or grantee name. Everything else is blank.
- **grant_type is my own judgement** from the title and grantee. Uncertain cases are noted in the CSV, for example Solutions for Our Climate Korea, "Alternative Protein R&D".
- **Dates are year and month only;** the site shows no day. Grants in the same month keep the site's order.
- **Experimental grant flag.** In the wide layout, one row (Ministry of Future Affairs, EU Regulatory Sandbox) shows an icon marking it as an "experimental grant". I checked pages 1 to 3 visually and saw no others; the last three rows of page 3 and the single row on page 4 were not checked in that layout.
- **has_published_research = unknown** means not found, not confirmed absent. Outputs were searched for the research and R&D grants only.
- **Outputs found but excluded** because I could not open them or the link was not established:
  - three further Purdue zein papers with the same FFAR award number (Rolandelli et al. 2024, Int J Biol Macromol, doi 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2024.135960; Rolandelli et al. 2025, Food Res Int, doi 10.1016/j.foodres.2025.115709; Ozturk et al. 2025, Food Hydrocolloids, doi 10.1016/j.foodhyd.2025.111635);
  - a Stanford lab menu experiment (Food Quality and Preference 2026, doi 10.1016/j.foodqual.2026.105931);
  - a second Breakthrough paper on crop prices (SSRN 4680226, page would not load);
  - a Solutions for Our Climate consumer survey (no funder named);
  - a CEEW dietary diversity release (not about alternative proteins);
  - Plant Based Foods Association retail pilot press coverage (no funder named).
- **No outputs found** for Carnegie (CG-G025), GFI Asia Pacific nutrition gaps (CG-G037), CEEW (CG-G041), the Berkeley Alternative Meats Lab (five grants) or IMPROVE (CG-G023).
- **Unconfirmed call details.** The official 2026 RFP page no longer loads. The award sizes and fish species list come from a background check that reports reading it earlier in this session, and I could not re-check them.
- **Background checks.** Output searches were run by background checks with web search and page fetches, not the browser. Each recorded output was opened on its own page or abstract by that check.
