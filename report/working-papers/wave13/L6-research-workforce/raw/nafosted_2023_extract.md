# NAFOSTED "Thông tin hoạt động tài trợ, hỗ trợ 2023" (Hanoi, November 2024): figures used by L6

Source: https://nafosted.gov.vn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Thong_tin_HD_taitro_hotro_2023_final.pdf (read 2026-10-15 via text extraction of the PDF; 4.8 MB file not kept here).
Vietnamese number format: the comma is the decimal mark, so "278,316 Tỷ Đ" is VND 278.316 billion.

- 284 projects funded from 2023; VND 278.316 billion approved for the 284 projects; VND 185.499 billion disbursed in 2023; 77 postdoctoral researchers supported; 892 valid proposals reviewed (999 applications reviewed in total on another slide).
- Basic research, natural sciences and engineering: 179 projects from 2023, VND 167.427 billion approved; 643 valid proposals reviewed.
- Basic research, social sciences and humanities: 76 projects, VND 62.069 billion.
- Average budget of one ordinary basic-research project: about VND 950 million ("~950 Tr.Đ"). Average budget of one strong-research-group project: about VND 3 billion ("~3 Tỷ Đ").
- Applied, potential and urgent research: 23 projects from 2023, VND 37.238 billion approved for 21 projects; average about VND 1.5 to 1.6 billion per project.
- International cooperation: 6 projects from 2023 (5 NAFOSTED-FWO, 1 NAFOSTED-DFG), VND 11.582 billion; average about VND 1.5 billion per project. Partner logos include NHMRC; other partner programmes named in the document: SNSF (Switzerland), DFG (Germany), FWO (Flanders), United Kingdom research bodies.
- Basic-research projects closed in 2023 produced 954 Web of Science publications and trained about 200 doctorates.
- NAFOSTED's state budget allocation in 2023 was 2.3% of Vietnam's recurrent state S&T budget estimate (slide 8).

Our conversions at 26,000 VND per USD (our calculation): VND 950 million = about USD 36,500; VND 3 billion = about USD 115,000; VND 1.5 to 1.6 billion = about USD 58,000 to 62,000; VND 278.316 billion = about USD 10.7 million.
