# L6: research system and workforce, GFI solutions screened for Vietnam

Line: L6 research system and workforce (wave 13). Source prefix: SLW. Date: 2026-10-15.

Files in this folder: `sources.csv` (20 new sources, SLW-01 to SLW-20), `data_solution_screen.csv` (14 GFI entries), `data_candidates.csv` (no rows), `data_research_workforce_facts.csv` (22 rows), `open_questions.csv` (6), `disagreements.csv` (3), `key_numbers.csv` (4), `changes.csv` (15), `calc_l6.py` (all calculations), `raw/openalex_queries.md` and `raw/nafosted_2023_extract.md` (the figures the paper relies on, with reproduce links).

Terms used: GFI is the Good Food Institute, an advocacy organisation; its Solutions Database is GSD-01. NAFOSTED is Vietnam's National Foundation for Science and Technology Development, under the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST). VINIF is the Vingroup Innovation Foundation. MOET is the Ministry of Education and Training; MOIT the Ministry of Industry and Trade. FIRI is MOIT's Food Industries Research Institute. HUST is Hanoi University of Science and Technology; IUH the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City. RM-09 is the study's no-regret move on process-engineering skills; RM-04 is shared food-grade pilot capacity; RM-11 a small permanent protein-innovation fund; T5 the shared pilot and tolling fermentation play.

## Verdict

None of the 14 GFI entries on research and workforce earns a new play or public good for Vietnam: 6 strengthen existing moves (mostly RM-09), 7 are not for Vietnam now, and 1 is already covered. The useful yield is a **constraint** and a **cost scale**. Hands-on fermentation training needs pilot-scale kit, but the study starts the skills move (RM-09) in 2027 and the pilot line (RM-04) only by 2030. So until 2030 the hands-on part must run on industrial tanks or abroad. NAFOSTED's own figures also show that the study's "small" protein fund (RM-11, USD 5 to 15 million a year) would equal about half to one and a half times everything NAFOSTED approved for new projects in 2023 (our calculation). The line also closes OQ-387, partly closes OQ-054 and OQ-126, and confirms clean negatives: no alternative-protein course, major, research centre, student group or event in Vietnam was found. Yield class: **added a constraint**, with **closed a question** as a second yield.

## Headline findings

1. **No alternative-protein education exists in Vietnam that we could find.** GFI's ecosystem database, re-checked on 15 October 2026, lists 2 Vietnamese companies and 0 researchers, courses, student groups, networks or research centres. The Alt Protein Project had 70 student chapters in 21 countries in August 2024, none in Vietnam. GFI's database is also thin for Thailand (0 courses, 0 centres), so it understates the region. [VN-direct, Medium, VCO-21; SLW-03; SLW-04; SLW-05]
2. **NAFOSTED's grant sizes are now known.** In 2023 it approved VND 278.3 billion (about USD 10.7 million) for 284 new projects across all fields. An ordinary basic-research project averaged about VND 950 million (about USD 36,500) and a strong-research-group project about VND 3 billion (about USD 115,000) (our conversions at 26,000 VND per USD). [VN-direct, High, SLW-01]
3. **NAFOSTED is the main domestic funder of Vietnamese food-protein research.** On one OpenAlex query of 525 Vietnam-affiliated food-protein works (2015 to 2026), NAFOSTED is acknowledged on 36 and VINIF on 5 (Vingroup JSC on 7 more). Korean and Taiwanese funders appear on 11 and 10, a sign of joint posts or researchers abroad. This closes OQ-387. [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-02]
4. **International alternative-protein money has not reached Vietnam.** None of 270 GFI-funded works in OpenAlex has a Vietnamese author (authors span 39 countries; Thailand also 0). GFI's grant programme was still closed on 15 October 2026. [general, Medium, SLW-02; RD-02]
5. **The study's protein fund is large by Vietnamese standards.** RM-11's USD 5 to 15 million a year equals VND 130 to 390 billion: about 47% to 140% of NAFOSTED's 2023 approvals for new projects, or 70% to 210% of its 2023 disbursements of VND 185.5 billion (our calculation). A first directed call at NAFOSTED scale, 10 ordinary or 3 strong-group projects, would cost about VND 9 to 10 billion (about USD 0.35 million) (our calculation). [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-01, estimate 2027]
6. **Decision 1002 gives RM-09 a named vehicle for senior engineers, not for operators.** MOET's Official Letter 5695 (17 September 2025) asks universities to build postgraduate biotechnology programmes on a "CNSH+X" model (biotechnology plus an application field), with requested state lab investment, through an annual proposal cycle. The decision's summary has no vocational or operator component. [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-08; SLW-07]
7. **We found no published fermentation short course for industry.** FIRI has a fermentation department, an enzyme and protein department and an industrial microbiology centre, but its training page lists doctoral training (latest notice 2022) and one 2018 analysis course. A 2013 framework for intermediate-level fermentation technicians exists on paper (snippet only). [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-14; INF-01] [VN-direct, Low, SLW-20]
8. **Course fees are small; access to kit is the binding cost.** A 21-hour hands-on fermentation engineering course at North Carolina State University costs USD 3,100, half of it at a pilot-scale stirred-tank bioreactor. A first cohort of 20 Vietnamese engineers would cost about USD 62,000 in fees, travel excluded (our calculation). Domestic food-industry short courses cost VND 3.2 to 4.2 million for 18 to 24 hours, about one twenty-second of the US rate per hour, but none covers fermentation. [general, High, SLW-09] [VN-direct, Low, SLW-16]
9. **IUH is the most visible Vietnamese meat-analogue group.** Since 2018 only 69 Vietnam-affiliated records match alternative-protein terms in OpenAlex, with at most 6 at any institution. IUH has two 2026 meat-analogue papers (a high-moisture analogue with jackfruit fibre, and a jackfruit pate). It is the natural host for a texturisation module. [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-02; TIC-21] [VN-direct, Low, SLW-18]
10. **Vietnam has hosted EU-funded food curriculum reform before.** The Erasmus+ NutriSEA project (2015 to 2018, with Hue University and HUST) opened a Bachelor in Food Safety at Hue and updated a Master's in Food Technology, alongside the AsiFood fermentation network. This is the delivery model GFI's course and programme entries assume. [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-13; RD-56]

## Detailed findings

### 1. University centres, programmes, majors and courses (GFI-022, GFI-023, GFI-024)

**What is taught now.** Food technology, food engineering and biotechnology degrees are large and widespread. Can Tho University alone has 300 food technology and 240 biotechnology places in 2026 [VN-direct, High, INF-15]. Search snippets give HUST 2026 quotas of 360 for food engineering, 160 or 140 for bioengineering (two pages disagree) and 60 for each of two advanced programmes [VN-direct, Low, SLW-19]. The HUST pages returned HTTP 503 when fetched, so these figures stay out of `talent.csv` for now (DG in `disagreements.csv`). Fermentation is taught inside food technology and bioengineering curricula, not as a degree of its own. We did not establish module content at any university.

**Alternative-protein content.** We found no alternative-protein major, minor, certificate, course or research centre in Vietnam [VN-direct, Medium, VCO-21]. Searches in English and Vietnamese ("protein thay thế", "thịt thực vật", "thịt nuôi cấy" with university, seminar and student terms) found none. A commercial market-report snippet claims Vietnamese universities are setting up cellular-agriculture programmes; it has no traceable source and is not used (see `disagreements.csv`).

**Research concentration.** Vietnamese research on alternative-protein terms is scattered: 69 Vietnam-affiliated records since 2018, at most 6 at any one institution (Duy Tan, IUH, Nong Lam, HCMUT, HUST and others) [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-02]. IUH has the only recent meat-analogue papers, both from 2026 [VN-direct, Medium, TIC-21] [VN-direct, Low, SLW-18].

**The state vehicle.** Decision 1002/QĐ-TTg (24 May 2025) sets STEM and digital graduate targets, 100 talent engineer and master's programmes, 100 talent PhD programmes and, for 2030 to 2035, at least 50 recognised strong research groups (30 in strategic technologies). It is funded from the state budget, institutions and enterprises; MOET, MOST and the Ministry of Finance lead [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-07]. The biotechnology graduate target already in the package (5,000 a year by 2030) comes from ECF-41. MOET's Official Letter 5695 asked universities to submit talent-programme proposals by 15 October 2025, with priority for postgraduate biotechnology on a "CNSH+X" model, and with requested state-budget lab investment; progress reports are due each 30 November [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-08].

**Reading.** A dedicated alternative-protein centre or major is not justified: output is too thin and the job base too small (a wave 3 estimate puts a 10,000 t a year plant at 60 to 100 staff, Low). But a "biotechnology plus food process engineering" talent master's under Decision 1002 is a realistic, already-funded route for RM-09's senior engineers. Courses should be short specialist modules (texturisation at IUH; side-stream protein and fermentation at HUST; food technology at Can Tho) that reuse open material rather than new degrees.

### 2. Student groups, conference sessions, events, outreach and school programmes (GFI-028, GFI-021, GFI-004, GFI-091, GFI-082)

- **Student groups.** None in Vietnam (above). Malaysia has one (Universiti Sains Malaysia) [general, Medium, SLW-03; SLW-05].
- **Events.** We found no alternative-protein event held in Vietnam in 2025 to 2026. Fi Vietnam, the main food-ingredient fair (Ho Chi Minh City; 300+ exhibitors and 9,000+ visitors expected; next edition 17 to 19 May 2028), lists no plant-protein, alternative-protein or fermentation theme [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-11]. Vietnam attended a two-day WHO Western Pacific and GFI workshop on alternative-protein regulation in 2021 (agency not named) [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-12]. Humane World's 2025 chef and nutritionist training is already in the package (AFN-23).
- **Outreach to scientists and engineers.** The realistic source of fermentation engineers is adjacent industry: 12 GMP-WHO veterinary vaccine plants (IND-34), recombinant vaccine makers and the amino-acid, yeast and brewing fermenters (app-s8 S8.7). Foreign funders on Vietnamese food-protein papers (Korea 11, Taiwan 10) point to researchers in joint posts or abroad (OQ-056) [VN-direct, Medium, SLW-02].
- **GFI APAC.** We found no GFI APAC event or programme in Vietnam. Its talent work is about Singapore: more than 180 operators, texturisation experts and formulators needed for plant-based meat by 2030, more than 500 technical hires projected by fermentation and cultivated startups, and a 2022 memorandum (NTU, Singapore Polytechnic, SIT, GFI APAC, Kerry, SGProtein, Buhler) proposing an apprenticeship with extrusion workshops [VN-adjacent, Low, SLW-10]. The transfer assumption: a Vietnamese version would need an extrusion line first.

**Reading.** These are cheap and can happen whenever students or researchers want them, but none is a reason to act at policy level. The one useful element is outreach to adjacent-industry engineers, which belongs inside RM-09.

### 3. Industry training and vocational programmes (GFI-054, GFI-068), against RM-09

**Who trains fermentation operators and process engineers now.**

| Level | Who | What we confirmed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineers and scientists | Universities (Can Tho, HUST, IUH and others) | Food technology, food engineering and bioengineering degrees; fermentation inside them | INF-15 [VN-direct, High]; SLW-19 [VN-direct, Low] |
| Doctoral researchers | FIRI | Doctoral training (latest admissions notice June 2022); departments of fermentation technology and of enzyme and protein technology; industrial microbiology centre | SLW-14; INF-01 [VN-direct, Medium] |
| Technicians | Colleges | A 2013 intermediate-level framework for fermentation technicians (operating beer, alcohol, ethanol and traditional fermentation equipment); whether any college teaches it now is unknown | SLW-20 [VN-direct, Low] |
| Technicians | MOIT Food Industry College (Viet Tri, Phu Tho) | Public college under MOIT training technical staff; its programmes are not listed on the MOIT page; the domain the ministry lists now shows a short-course centre with food-safety, HACCP, testing and small-scale processing courses and no fermentation course | SLW-17 [VN-direct, Medium]; SLW-16 [VN-direct, Low] |
| Operators | The fermenters themselves | We found no published source on in-house training at Vedan, Ajinomoto, CJ or breweries | none: open question |
| Postgraduate abroad | Ajinomoto Scholarship Foundation | ASEAN+One scholarship in Japan since 2009 for science students from 6 countries; about 16 Vietnamese recipients (count ambiguous) | SLW-15 [VN-direct, Low] |

**Cost.** Fees for short training are small. A hands-on fermentation engineering course in the United States costs USD 3,100 for 21 hours, about USD 148 an hour [general, High, SLW-09]. Domestic food-industry short courses cost about VND 175,000 an hour (VND 3.2 to 4.2 million for 18 to 24 hours), about 22 times less per hour, but none covers fermentation [VN-direct, Low, SLW-16] (our calculation, `calc_l6.py`). A first cohort of 20 engineers on a course of the US type would cost about USD 62,000 in fees, travel excluded (our calculation). This is less than two ordinary NAFOSTED grants.

**The constraint.** GFI's own entry asks to "Build exemplar manufacturing, R&D, and processing facilities for training and research" (GSD-01, GFI-054), and the US benchmark spends half its hours at a pilot-scale bioreactor. We found no open food-grade pilot fermenter in Vietnam (ch06 6.4; app-s8 S8.1). Chapter 28 sequences RM-09 for 2027 and RM-04 by 2030 (ch28 28.5). So between 2027 and 2030 the hands-on part of RM-09 can only run on the industrial tanks of tolling hosts (Vedan, Ajinomoto, Daesang, as named under T5) or abroad. The senior-engineer track can start in 2027 through Decision 1002; the operator track depends on tolling hosts agreeing to take trainees. RM-09's lead actors (MOET, MOST, universities, industry) omit MOIT, which runs FIRI and the food colleges.

**Reading.** RM-09 is the right move; it is not yet a costed or owned programme. It should be split into a senior-engineer track (MOET talent programmes under Decision 1002) and an operator track (MOIT colleges and FIRI with tolling hosts), and its 2027 start should be read as classroom and placement work until pilot kit exists.

Its state allocation was 2.3% of Vietnam's recurrent state science budget estimate for 2023. VINIF's site shows programme categories and 2018 to 2023 totals; the latest dated programme announcement is 20 December 2024 and no 2025 or 2026 call is shown [VN-direct, Medium, RD-05].

**International.** GFI's research funding database (titled 2021) shows no Vietnamese funder or eligibility text on the page we could read; its listings did not load [general, Low, SLW-06]. GFI's grant programme remained closed on 15 October 2026, with planning-grant awards expected later in 2026 [general, High, RD-02]. No GFI-funded work has a Vietnamese author [general, Medium, SLW-02]. The Coefficient Giving request (up to USD 30 million) closed on 15 September 2026 (CAP-57, already in the package). We found no open call from the Bezos Centre at the National University of Singapore for researchers outside Singapore (search snippets only, not used as a finding).

**Reading.** Vietnam does not need its own funding database: `research_funding.csv` already does the job and now gains sizes. The useful finding is scale. RM-11 was sized on Danish, German and Canadian budgets; against NAFOSTED it is large. A first directed call through NAFOSTED of about VND 9 to 10 billion would test demand from Vietnamese researchers at a size Vietnam already runs, and NAFOSTED's joint calls with FWO, DFG and SNSF are a ready route for foreign co-funding.

### 5. Academic-industry collaboration and makerspaces (GFI-057, GFI-080, GFI-081)

Industry-academic links in food fermentation are thin in the public record: the Ajinomoto scholarship above, EU projects that tied curricula to enterprise internships (SLW-13), and the academic partners already named in the plays (the VNU Hanoi fungal group for T9; FIRI for T1, T5 and T10). GFI's researcher directory lists no Vietnamese researchers (VCO-21); `institutions.csv` already serves as the Vietnamese directory. A public plant-protein makerspace adds little; its firm-facing core, a shared extrusion trial line for small *chay* (traditional vegetarian food) makers, is already proposed in ch06 6.7 and tracked in OQ-310 and OQ-386. A public cultivated-meat makerspace is premature: Vietnam has no food cell-line work and T8 is a research-only play.

### Deep evaluation and candidates

Shortlist: GFI-054 and GFI-068 (training, section 3), GFI-023 (programmes through Decision 1002, section 1) and GFI-015 (funding, section 4). Each strengthens an existing move, RM-09 or RM-11; none fills a gap that no existing play, public good or move covers, and none would beat an existing play on any of the seven criteria as a stand-alone item. We therefore propose **no new candidates**; `data_candidates.csv` has a header only. A directed NAFOSTED call is better treated as a policy option or a first step of RM-11 than as a play.

## Next-wave candidates

1. **Tolling hosts as training hosts (deeper, not desk).** Would Vedan, Ajinomoto, a yeast maker or a brewery take outside trainees or let a training cohort use tank time, and at what fee? This decides whether RM-09 can have hands-on content before 2030. Phone calls only; desk research cannot close it.
2. **MOET talent programmes (different kind, partly desk).** Did any university win a "biotechnology plus food or bioprocess" talent programme in the 2025 cycle, with how much lab money? MOET decisions may be published; otherwise ask MOET. A yes would give RM-09 a host and possibly co-fund pilot kit.
3. **A NAFOSTED directed call (different kind, not desk).** Can NAFOSTED run a thematic call on protein from Vietnamese side streams and species under Law 93/2025, with foreign co-funding? Ask NAFOSTED. A yes turns ch06 6.7 and RM-11 into a costed first step.
4. **Regional hands-on places (deeper, partly desk).** Would Thai or Singapore pilot facilities accept Vietnamese trainees, and at what cost? Email their training offices.
5. **Operator wages (OQ-083, not desk).** No salary survey for process engineers or operators was found again in this wave; the recruiters' 2026 guides need a request.

Further desk work on this line would be noise. Admissions pages repeat the wave 2 failures (HUST returned 503; IUH and others publish quotas in images), and GFI's databases are near-empty for the whole region. The remaining questions need calls to companies, MOET and NAFOSTED.

## Limits

- **WebSearch:** 22 of the 25 calls allowed; about 85 tool calls in total. Search results were thin for Vietnamese-language education and event pages.
- **Failed or blocked fetches (not retried by other means):** HUST pages ts.hust.edu.vn (503), hust.edu.vn (503; www.hust.edu.vn DNS failure) and smse.hust.edu.vn (503); thuvienphapluat.vn text of Decision 1002 (403); businessinfo.cz page on Fi Vietnam (403); the LawNet page on Circular 17/2013 (redirected to an unrelated page); the WUR page on a Southeast Asian food-training network (404); gfi.org/alt-protein-project/ (404). The GFI research funding database listings did not load.
- **OpenAlex:** the public API returned HTTP 429 (daily budget exhausted), so we used the OpenAlex connector. Funder metadata is incomplete; all counts are lower bounds. The alternative-protein term search includes off-topic records.
- **Snippet-only evidence** (Low): HUST quotas (SLW-19) and the fermentation technician framework (SLW-20). The fic.edu.vn short-course page (SLW-16) may not be run by the MOIT college that the ministry lists at that domain.
- **Not established:** in-house operator training at Vietnamese fermenters; current intake of any fermentation technician programme; engineer and operator wages; VINIF 2025 to 2026 calls; module content of Vietnamese food technology and bioengineering degrees.
- **NAFOSTED figures** are approvals for projects starting in 2023 (multi-year budgets) and disbursements in 2023. Comparing them with an annual fund (RM-11) is a scale check, not a like-for-like budget comparison.
- We named institutions and programmes only, not individual students or staff.
