# R1. Open questions register

This register lists every question the research could not close. It is generated from `open_questions.csv`, which also holds the source notes each question came from. The twenty questions that matter most, with the play each unblocks, are ranked in [[ch30-unknowns]]; their rank appears in the "Priority" column below. Version 0.2 adds the questions left by the futures round (OQ-128 onwards, topic "futures 2030 to 2050"); the most important are summarised in [[ch28-robust-moves]]. Version 0.3 adds the questions left by the demand round (OQ-156 onwards, topics beginning "demand"), with the priority each research stream gave them (High, Medium or Low); the ones that most change Part III are discussed in [[ch25-demand-to-frontier]]. Version 0.4 adds the questions left by the actor check and expansion waves 7 to 9 (OQ-270 to OQ-376; see [[app-m4-actor-check-waves]]) and updates the status of several demand questions. Version 0.7 adds the questions left by the citation check (OQ-377 onwards).

Status: 411 open; 31 partly closed; 4 closed.

Readers who can answer any of these are invited to contact AltProtein Vietnam; closed questions will be dated in the data file.
