# D5. Business buyers of protein ingredients

**What this appendix contains.** The data behind [[ch16-business-buyers]]. Vietnam's own reported quantities for these customs lines are flagged as estimated by UN Comtrade, so values (CIF) are the better Vietnam-side measure; partner-reported (mirror) exports give tonnages and a series to 2025 at FOB values [@BUY-01; @BUY-02] {VN-direct|High}. No customs line maps cleanly to one ingredient, and the split between food and feed is not visible. It also gives landed prices at 0% preferential duty, the duty schedule, the monthly timing of Chinese shipments (D5.2), seven buyers and intermediaries found by the actor check and waves 7 to 9 (D5.3), and updated segment specifications (D5.4) ([[app-m4-actor-check-waves]]).
