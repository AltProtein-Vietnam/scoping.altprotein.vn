# 24. Vietnam 2050: a vision for a measured, efficient and diversified protein supply

**In one paragraph.** Chapters 20 to 23 explore what might happen. This chapter says what could be aimed for. It sets out one normative vision for Vietnam's protein supply in 2050 and works backwards to milestones in each five-year plan. The vision is deliberately led by feed security and industry, the framing that has proved most durable abroad, rather than by diet change, which is outside this study's scope. It asks for five things: measure the protein balance; economise on imported protein through feed efficiency and formulation; diversify sources with microbial protein and non-marine omega-3; specialise in the regional niches where Vietnam has an edge; and decarbonise and climate-proof the new capacity. Every number is a range drawn from Vietnam's own 2050 plans, from the balance model and from what other countries have achieved. No country we checked has a protein plan that reaches 2050; this one would be unusually long-range, and it should be reviewed at each five-year plan.

> **Vision, not forecast.** Everything in this chapter tagged *vision* is a goal that AltProtein Vietnam puts forward for discussion, not a prediction and not an official target. The benchmark evidence behind each goal is cited and tagged as usual. The ranges come from [[ch22-protein-balance-2050]]; their fit with each 2050 world is tested in section 24.7.

## 24.1 Why a vision, and why this framing

- **Nobody has a protein plan to 2050.** Protein horizons abroad mostly stop at 2030 to 2035: the EU, Canada and Singapore at 2035, Japan's basic plan at FY2030, and China, Israel and the Netherlands at 2030. Japan's MIDORI strategy (2050) and Denmark's land deal (2045) reach further but carry no protein indicator [@VIS-15; @VIS-08; @VIS-25; @VIS-13; @GEO-10; @VIS-23; @VIS-05; @VIS-49; @VIS-03] {general|Medium}. The one exception we found is a Japanese food-tech investment roadmap to 2040, reported only in the press, with about JPY 1 trillion for novel foods [@VIS-14] {VN-adjacent|Low}.
- **Vietnam's official 2050 plans cover inputs, not protein.** Non-hydro renewables at 74 to 75% of power, 10 to 20 Mt of clean hydrogen a year, an agricultural emissions cap of 56 MtCO2e and net zero are all official 2050 numbers; no official meat, feed or protein number goes beyond 2030 [@GT-11; @NTS-25; @NTS-24; @CLM-01; @NTS-07] {VN-direct|High} {fx:projection} (official targets). A protein vision can anchor on those inputs.
- **Security and industry framing lead, with climate as a co-benefit.** The EU's July 2026 plan is titled for resilience and strategic autonomy; Japan and Korea state targets as food security; Singapore left alternative protein out of its food-security targets for cost and acceptance reasons [@VIS-15; @VIS-13; @VIS-29; @VIS-25] {general|Medium} {fx:signal}.
- **Plans without a statistic cannot be judged.** The Dutch protein strategy published no quantitative targets, we found no published progress report for Canada's roadmap and Denmark's action plan has no indicators; Japan, the EU and China are tracked even when they miss [@VIS-05; @VIS-08; @VIS-10; @VIS-01; @VIS-11; @VIS-12; @VIS-13; @VIS-15; @GEO-11] {general|Medium}.
- **Backcasting works only when embedded.** Reviews name follow-up and monitoring as the weak link; a Dutch novel-protein backcast in the 1990s seeded a research programme, yet the country's 2020 strategy still published no quantified targets for its solution tracks [@VIS-37; @VIS-38; @VIS-41; @VIS-05] {general|Medium}.

## 24.2 The vision

> **Vision, not forecast.** By 2050 Vietnam feeds a larger and richer population, and supplies the world's shrimp and pangasius buyers, from a protein system that is **measured, efficient, diversified, specialised and low-carbon**. It publishes its protein and feed balance every year. It needs 7.0 to 8.8 Mt (about 7 to 9 Mt) of imported soybean meal instead of the 10.4 Mt that trend growth implies. It makes 4 to 8% of its high-protein feed protein from microbes, grown on Vietnamese carbohydrate and residues and, increasingly, on clean power. It sources 15 to 50% of its aquafeed omega-3 from algal and oilseed oils. It is a recognised regional supplier of contract fermentation and aquafeed functional ingredients. And it has the statistics, rules and pilot capacity to change course as the world changes. {fx:vision}

## 24.6 The map in 2050

The vision has a geography. Six candidate hubs open at different times ([[app-f5-targets-hubs]], `candidate_hubs.csv`) {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}:
- **Ho Chi Minh City with Dong Nai:** pilot plants, tolling and food-grade fermentation, now to 2035; in our assessment the only hub where most assets already operate, including Vedan's and Ajinomoto's fermentation plants in Dong Nai and Saigon Hi-Tech Park [@IND-01; @IND-04; @IND-08; @IND-57] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **The Mekong river belt (Dong Thap, Can Tho, An Giang):** feed ingredients and side streams from pangasius and rice, 2027 to 2035 [@HUB-13; @HUB-14; @HUB-28] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Tay Ninh (former Long An and Tay Ninh):** fermentation sugar from cassava, 2028 to 2040, with the lowest industrial-park occupancy in the south (60.9% in the second quarter of 2026) and a large land bank, but not enough roots to carry a national sugar route alone [@FS-03; @COST-21] {VN-direct|Low}.
- **The Mekong shrimp coast (Ca Mau, Vinh Long):** aquafeed plus hydrogen, demonstration 2032 to 2035 and scale 2040 to 2045, on raised sites that use surface water [@HUB-15; @HUB-05; @CLM-14] {VN-direct|Low}.
- **The north (Hanoi Biotechnology High-Tech Zone, Bac Tu Liem):** research now; industry in the 2040s (our judgement) [@HUB-19; @HUB-20] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **The south-central coast (Khanh Hoa and Lam Dong, including former Ninh Thuan and Binh Thuan):** the best long-run solar and shrimp-hatchery niches, 2035 to 2050 [@HUB-02; @HUB-23] {VN-direct|Low}.

## 24.7 How the vision fares in each world

| Pillar | A. Regional workshop | B. Comfortable price-taker | C. Security build-out | D. Squeezed importer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Measure | Achievable | Achievable | Achievable | Achievable |
| Economise | Achievable | Achievable | Achievable, and urgent | Achievable, and carries most of the load |
| Diversify | Lower half of the range | Bottom of the range, mostly imported | Upper half of the range | Lower half, partly imported |
| Specialise | Strongest | Weak | Strong | Narrow (local niches) |
| Decarbonise and adapt | Achievable | Achievable | Achievable if clean power is delivered | Achievable, at higher cost |

Our judgement from [[ch23-scenarios-2050]] {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. Three of the five pillars are achievable in every world. The diversification and specialisation pillars depend on the world, which is why their milestones are ranges and why the vision needs a review cycle.

## 24.8 What would make us revise it

- **Upwards:** fishmeal above USD 2,500 per t for 12 months; an ASEAN reliance mechanism for novel foods; delivered green hydrogen below USD 2 per kg in southern Vietnam; a working cellulosic sugar plant in Asia.
- **Downwards:** a sustained soy glut; Chinese export dumping of microbial protein; a serious novel-food safety scandal in Asia; no feed-protein balance published by 2030.
- **Review cycle:** at each five-year plan (2030, 2035, 2040, 2045), using the 16 signposts in `signposts_2050.csv` and the method in [[app-m2-futures-method]]. Vietnam's own foresight studies advise small, high-quality scenario workshops because statistics are thin [@VIS-45] {VN-direct|Low}. *Vietnam 2035*, written jointly by the World Bank and the planning ministry, is the precedent for a jointly authored national vision [@VIS-46] {VN-direct|Medium}.

## 24.9 What this means

- **Policy makers:** the 2026 to 2030 milestones are small, cheap and mostly administrative: a statistic, a route, a named site, a budget line and a pilot plant. The successor livestock and fisheries strategies (before 2030) are the natural home for protein-balance targets.
- **International bodies:** the balance sheet and the review cycle are where technical assistance adds most; the EU's new balance-sheet method is a ready template.
- **Investors:** the vision implies microbial feed-protein capacity of about nine times Entobel's current Vietnamese insect-meal capacity (11 kt a year of product) by 2030, comparing protein with protein (our calculation), and a regional contract-fermentation niche; the protein-from-power opportunity is a 2040s option.
- **Manufacturers:** the vision's largest single lever is yours: feed efficiency and formulation. Sharing anonymised inclusion and conversion data is the first step.
- **Startups and researchers:** the vision rewards process know-how on Vietnamese feedstocks, strains and species, not generic platforms.
