# 24. Vietnam 2050: a vision for a measured, more diverse and more secure protein supply

**In brief.**
- This chapter proposes one vision for the protein Vietnamese people eat in 2050, and works back to milestones in each five-year plan. It is a goal for discussion, not a forecast.
- In the vision, Vietnam feeds a larger, richer and older population from a protein supply that is measured, more diverse and more secure. Meat stays the main protein; new protein adds a second source.
- It has five pillars: measure, make, diversify, specialise and secure.
- By 2050, new routes supply about 67,000 to 172,000 t of protein a year, about 59,000 to 147,000 t of it made in Vietnam (our model).
- Most first steps are cheap. The largest is a public innovation fund of USD 5 to 15 million a year, which is large by Vietnamese standards [@SLW-01] {VN-direct|Medium}.

> **Vision, not forecast.** Everything here tagged *vision* is a goal AltProtein Vietnam puts forward for discussion, not a prediction or an official target. The ranges come from the demand model in [[ch18-demand-sizing]], on the same basis as [[ch22-protein-balance-2050]].

## 24.1 Why a vision, and why this framing

- **Nobody has a protein plan to 2050.** Protein plans abroad mostly stop at 2030 to 2035 [@VIS-15; @VIS-08; @VIS-25; @VIS-13; @GEO-10; @VIS-23; @VIS-05; @VIS-49; @VIS-03; @VIS-14] {general|Medium}.
- **Vietnam's official 2050 plans cover inputs, not protein:** renewable power, hydrogen and a farm emissions cap of 56 MtCO2e. No official meat, feed or protein number goes beyond 2030 [@GT-11; @NTS-25; @NTS-24; @CLM-01; @NTS-07] {VN-direct|High} {fx:projection} (official targets). A protein vision can anchor on those inputs.
- **Security and industry lead, with climate as a co-benefit.** The EU, Japan and Korea frame protein as security [@VIS-15; @VIS-13; @VIS-29; @VIS-25] {general|Medium} {fx:signal}. This vision follows that lead. Diet change is not a goal of this study.
- **Plans without a statistic cannot be judged.** Strategies with no measurable target, such as the Dutch and Danish ones, leave no way to track progress [@VIS-05; @VIS-08; @VIS-10; @VIS-01; @VIS-11; @VIS-12; @VIS-13; @VIS-15; @GEO-11] {general|Medium}.
- **Working back from a goal works only with follow-up.** Reviews name monitoring as the weak link [@VIS-37; @VIS-38; @VIS-41; @VIS-05] {general|Medium}.
- **Feed is context, not a goal.** Each tonne of meat protein replaced avoids about 4.5 t of imported soybean meal (our calculation, [[ch22-protein-balance-2050]]) {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. But at the scale demand supports, new protein adds a second source rather than changing national exposure {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}.

## 24.2 The vision

> **Vision, not forecast.** By 2050 Vietnam feeds a larger, richer and older population from a protein supply that is **measured, more diverse and more secure**. It publishes a yearly protein balance of what people eat and where it comes from. Domestic makers supply 40% to 60% of the plant-protein ingredients that food makers now import. {fx:vision}
>
> New routes supply about 67,000 to 172,000 t of protein a year: upgraded *chay* (vegetarian food), declared meat and plant blends, canteen dishes, plant drinks and fermented foods. About 59,000 to 147,000 t is made in Vietnam, mostly plant protein processed at home in place of imports, some grown on Vietnamese starch and side streams. {fx:vision}
>
> Vietnam has a working new-food route, open food-grade pilot plants and a protein-quality laboratory. It supplies fermentation sugar and contract fermentation to the region. Meat stays the main protein; new protein adds a second source. The vision is reviewed at every five-year plan. {fx:vision}

**The scale behind it.** On trend, population grows to about 110 million by 2050, and the meat protein people eat rises from about 1.01 Mt to 1.45 Mt {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. People aged 65 and over double to 20% [@GT-12; @GEO-31] {VN-direct|Medium} {fx:projection}. New protein displaces about 0.4% to 1.5% of meat protein by 2050 {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate} {dx:inferred}. That is why the vision speaks of a second source, not a replacement.

## 24.3 Five pillars

| Pillar | What it means by 2050 | Headline indicator | Why it is credible | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Measure** | A yearly national protein balance, by food, source and origin; accredited protein-quality testing; a public price series for protein ingredients {fx:vision} | Balance published every year from 2028 | Every tracked foreign target has a statistic; no Vietnamese laboratory offers protein-digestibility testing today | [@VIS-15; @VIS-13] {general\|Medium}; [@INF-03; @INF-10; @INF-11; @INF-13; @INF-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **Make** | Vietnam makes much more of the protein ingredients its food makers use {fx:vision} | 59,000 to 147,000 t of protein a year made in Vietnam; 40% to 60% of plant-protein ingredients from domestic makers | Imported textured soy, gluten and isolates are real demand today, mostly from China | [@BUY-02; @BRD-05; @BRD-06] {VN-direct\|Low} |
| **Diversify** | New routes add a second source of protein beside meat {fx:vision} | 67,000 to 172,000 t of protein a year from new routes | Each route is anchored on what has happened elsewhere ([[ch18-demand-sizing]]) | [@GLB-09] {VN-direct\|Low} {fx:estimate} |
| **Specialise** | Regional niches: cassava fermentation sugar, contract fermentation, high-value fermented ingredients; cultivated seafood as research {fx:vision} | Named regional products and sites | Existing fermentation plants; Vietnam is China's largest cassava starch supplier but sells it almost no glucose | [@IND-01; @FS-01; @FS-13; @RGN-51; @RGN-55] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **Secure** | Protein supply less exposed to import and climate shocks; new plants on climate-proof sites with clean power {fx:vision} | 0.4% to 1.5% of meat protein displaced; agriculture within its emissions cap | The delta sinks about 1.1 cm a year; official 2050 cap | [@CLM-13; @CLM-14; @CLM-01; @GT-11] {VN-direct\|Medium} |

How to read the model rows {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate} {dx:inferred}:
- **"Made in Vietnam" is generous.** On a strict reading, the 2035 benchmark figure is about 11,400 t, not 16,600 t ([[ch18-demand-sizing]]).
- **Most of it is import replacement,** about two-thirds in 2050. It displaces no meat; its value is the import bill, processing capacity and jobs.
- **The stretch path is a stretch,** the top of a range, not a target.
- **Meat displaced is not a diet target.** Existing chay days already avoid more meat than either path adds.

## 24.5 Working backwards: milestones by five-year plan

The milestones follow Vietnam's planning cycle, so each plan can adopt, adjust or drop them. Most early ones are administrative and cheap.

| Plan period | Milestones {fx:vision} | What makes them plausible |
|---|---|---|
| **2026 to 2030** | A national protein balance by 2028. A new-food clause in the Food Safety Law before the May 2027 vote, then a procedure with deadlines and reliance on trusted regulators [@APR-07] {VN-direct\|Medium}. Food-fermentation sites named in the national biotechnology cluster plan. A food-protein innovation fund. A siting rule for climate-proof plants. A protein-quality laboratory and price series. One open food-grade pilot plant of 10,000 to 30,000 L. | Foreign budgets and pilot plants; Korea changed a rule in about nine months [@VIS-15; @VIS-01; @VIS-24; @VIS-06; @RGN-13; @RGN-36; @HUB-09; @NTS-37; @RGN-19; @RGN-21; @RGN-34] {general\|Medium}; delta subsidence [@CLM-13; @CLM-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |

## 24.6 The map in 2050

The vision has a geography of candidate hubs, each a cluster of plants in one area {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}.
- **Ho Chi Minh City with Dong Nai:** pilot plants, tolling and food-grade fermentation, now to 2035. It is the only hub where most assets already operate, including Vedan's and Ajinomoto's plants [@IND-01; @IND-04; @IND-08; @IND-57] {VN-direct|Medium}. It suits plays T1, T5 and T7.
- **Tay Ninh:** fermentation sugar from cassava (play T4), 2028 to 2040. It has spare industrial land and ample cassava [@FS-03; @COST-21] {VN-direct|Low}.
- **The Mekong river belt (Dong Thap, Can Tho, An Giang):** rice side streams for koji and fungal foods (T9) and rice protein for drinks (T10), 2027 to 2035 [@HUB-13; @HUB-14; @HUB-28] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Hanoi's biotechnology zone:** research now, industry in the 2040s. A home for the protein-quality laboratory and cultivated seafood research (T8) [@HUB-19; @HUB-20] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **The south-central coast (Khanh Hoa, Lam Dong):** the best long-run solar site, with small seaweed and spirulina niches [@HUB-02] {VN-direct|Low}.
- **The Mekong shrimp coast: context only,** since its case rests on fish feed [@HUB-15; @HUB-05; @CLM-14] {VN-direct|Low}.

## 24.7 How the vision fares in each world

| Pillar | A. Regional workshop | B. Comfortable price-taker | C. Security build-out | D. Squeezed importer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Measure | Achievable | Achievable | Achievable | Achievable |
| Make | Upper half of the range | Near the drift path: imports enter duty-free [@FTR-32] {VN-direct\|High} | Upper half | Lower half, in home niches |
| Diversify | Achievable | Achievable, with more imported ingredients | Achievable | Achievable, with more imported ingredients |
| Specialise | Strongest | Weak | Strong | Narrow |
| Secure | Achievable | Achievable, but the second source stays small | Achievable, and urgent | Achievable, at higher cost |

Our judgement from [[ch23-scenarios-2050]] {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. Measuring, diversifying and securing are achievable in every world, because they rest on public goods and on demand that the two axes do not decide. Making and specialising depend on the world. That is why their milestones are ranges, and why the vision needs reviews.

## 24.8 What would make us revise it

- **Upwards:** a Southeast Asian system to accept novel-food approvals; a Vietnamese processor selling a declared blend that buyers accept (none does today [@HXE-04; @HXE-09; @TRU-22; @TRU-07] {VN-direct|Medium} {dx:revealed}); a large caterer making a plant dish the default; price parity per serving; a working straw-sugar plant in Asia.
- **Downwards:** fast-growing protein imports from China; Chinese dumping of microbial protein; a novel-food safety scandal; no protein balance by 2030; no new-food procedure by 2028.
- **Review cycle:** at each five-year plan, using the signposts in [[ch23-scenarios-2050]]. Small, high-quality scenario workshops suit Vietnam's thin statistics [@VIS-45] {VN-direct|Low}. *Vietnam 2035*, written jointly by the World Bank and the planning ministry, is the precedent for a shared national vision [@VIS-46] {VN-direct|Medium}.

## 24.9 What this means

- **Policy makers:** the 2026 to 2030 milestones are small and cheap: a statistic, a route, named sites, a budget line, a laboratory and a pilot plant. The most urgent is the new-food clause before the May 2027 vote [@APR-07] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **International bodies:** help most with the protein balance and the review cycle. The EU's new method is a ready template [@VIS-15] {general|Medium}.
- **Investors:** the vision is an ingredient business first, worth roughly USD 200 million a year by 2050 on the benchmark path, two-thirds of it import replacement {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate} {dx:inferred}.
- **Manufacturers:** most of the vision's volume is your buying. Start with soy flour quotes, conditional orders from chay makers, and tests of extenders against your soy recipe.
- **Startups and researchers:** the vision rewards know-how on Vietnamese starch, side streams, strains and species. A protein-quality dataset for local ingredients is a cheap early contribution ([[ch06-knowledge-talent]]).
