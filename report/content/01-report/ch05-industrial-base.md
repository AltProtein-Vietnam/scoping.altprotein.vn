# 5. The industrial base: fermentation and food processing

**In brief.**
- Vietnam's industry is strong where food-protein makers need buyers and co-packers, and thin where they need process equipment.
- Frozen-food makers already make vegetarian lines next to meat, and store brands show that contract manufacturing of vegetarian food works.
- The sterile, air-fed fermentation tanks that new protein needs sit almost entirely inside three amino-acid makers. None offers contract manufacturing.
- We found no food-grade contract fermentation, no open pilot fermenter, no protein-isolate plant and no Vietnamese maker of textured soy.
- Soybean crushing is growing fast, but only for animal feed.

---

## 5.1 Fermentation: large in volume, captive in practice

**Aerobic fermentation** uses stirred, aerated, sterile tanks. It makes amino acids, yeast and microbial protein. In Vietnam it is concentrated in three firms:

| Firm | Location | What it makes | Size signals | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vedan Vietnam (Taiwan) | Dong Nai | Monosodium glutamate (MSG), lysine, starch, glucose syrup | Last published glutamic acid capacity 180,000 t a year (2004); Vietnam revenue USD 154 M in 2025, down 7% | [@VCO-03; @IND-01; @VCO-01] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| Ajinomoto Vietnam (Japan) | Dong Nai | MSG from cassava starch and molasses | 150,000 t a year (2023) | [@IND-08; @IND-09] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| Daesang Vietnam (Korea) | Phu Tho; starch in Tay Ninh | MSG and starch | Over 35,000 t a year of seasoning (2020) | [@IND-11; @IND-13] {VN-direct\|Low} |

We estimate Vietnam's sterile, air-fed fermentation volume at **about 11,000 to 34,000 m3**, almost all inside these three firms (our estimate from published capacities) {VN-direct|Low}. Breweries and ethanol plants add far more volume, but their tanks are not aerated.

**Other fermentation assets:**
- **Baker's yeast:** two small makers, AB Mauri and Lesaffre [@IND-15; @IND-16; @IND-17] {VN-direct|Medium}. Yeast is the closest existing process to microbial protein.
- **Fuel ethanol:** only two or three plants ran stably in mid-2026, and the new E10 petrol mandate is pulling idle plants back to fuel [@IND-18; @IND-19; @IND-20; @SLF-13] {VN-direct|Medium}. With fuel paying this well, no ethanol plant is a realistic protein retrofit before 2030 (our inference) {VN-direct|Low}.
- **Breweries:** large, and a source of food-grade side streams and trained staff, not of aerated tanks [@IND-23; @IND-26; @IND-27] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Vaccines:** Vietnam's vaccine plants offer quality systems and skills, not tank volume [@IND-34; @IND-37; @IND-31; @IND-32] {VN-direct|Medium}.

**No contract fermentation.** We found no Vietnamese company offering food-grade contract fermentation, and no public facility with an open pilot service [@IND-01; @IND-08; @INF-02; @INF-04; @INF-05] {VN-direct|Medium}. The national food research institute's pilot centre has no fermenters [@INF-02] {VN-direct|High}. One small food fermenter, YERA in Nghe An, makes yeast protein, but publishes no scale and makes no contract offer [@RDL-01] {VN-direct|Low}.

Vedan's falling MSG sales and replacement-only investment [@VCO-01] suggest the incumbents may have spare capacity and a motive to talk. That is a hypothesis, not a finding. This gap is why shared pilot and tolling fermentation (play T5) is one of the three leading plays ([[ch26-plays]]).

## 5.2 Feed mills: large and half used, but context only

In 2023 Vietnam had 269 industrial feed mills, running at about half their capacity [@IND-39] {VN-direct|Medium}. Feed matters here only as the hidden import behind the meat, eggs and fish people eat. Feed mills are not partners for this study's plays.

## 5.3 Soy and protein-ingredient processing

- **Crushing.** A Bunge and Wilmar plant near Ho Chi Minh City can crush 2.6 Mt of soybeans a year since a second line opened in 2025 [@VCO-16; @FORM-10] {VN-direct|Medium}. National crush may reach 3.0 Mt in 2027 [@MAC-04] {VN-direct|Medium}. **No food-grade soy flour or soy-protein line is on record.**
- **Protein isolates and concentrates.** We found no soy, pea or rice protein isolate plant in Vietnam. Distributors sell Chinese soy protein and wheat gluten in 20 to 25 kg bags [@FORM-11; @FORM-13] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Textured soy.** No Vietnamese maker was found. Chinese extrusion lines are marketed in Vietnam [@FORM-34; @FORM-35] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Soy milk and tofu.** Vinasoy can make 390 million litres of soy milk a year and claims about 80% of the market [@IND-48; @IND-49; @IND-50] {VN-direct|Medium}. Tofu is mostly made in small workshops; one factory, ToFu House, is building fields of domestic soybeans [@IND-51; @FORM-14] {VN-direct|Medium}.

**What this means for food.** Food-grade soy flour from the growing crush sector is the obvious input for domestic textured plant protein (play T1). Nobody makes it yet.

## 5.4 Food processors and co-packers

- **Meat and frozen-food makers with vegetarian lines:** Vissan, CJ Foods Vietnam, Cholimex and SG Food make *đồ chay* (traditional vegetarian food) on the same lines as meat [@VCO-13; @BUY-04; @VCO-12; @FORM-27; @FORM-31] {VN-direct|Medium}. They are the most plausible co-packers for plant-based products.
- **Store brands:** Saigon Co.op's vegetarian lines are made under contract [@FORM-01] {VN-direct|Medium}. So chay contract manufacturing already works.
- **Dairy:** Vinamilk, TH and Nutifood all sell plant milks [@ECO-43; @ECO-45; @AIB-10] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Glucose:** Vedan sells cassava glucose syrup to other firms [@VCO-03; @VCO-05; @IND-11] {VN-direct|Medium}. It is the obvious local carbon source for food fermentation, and it already exists.

## 5.5 Shared infrastructure and services

- **Parks:** Saigon Hi-Tech Park and Hoa Lac Hi-Tech Park in Hanoi have research zones and incubators [@INF-06; @INF-25] {VN-direct|Medium}.
- **Testing:** several accredited food laboratories exist, including the Ministry of Health's National Institute for Food Control [@INF-03; @INF-10; @INF-13] {VN-direct|High}. But no laboratory we found offers the protein-digestibility test that a protein-quality claim or novel-food application needs ([[ch06-knowledge-talent]]).
- **Engineering:** foreign firms typically design plants and local contractors fit them out [@IND-31; @IND-64; @INF-24] {VN-direct|Medium}. We found no local maker of food-grade fermenters, extruders or spray dryers.

**For each reader.**
- **Investors:** do not assume rentable fermentation capacity in Vietnam. Treat Vedan and Ajinomoto as possible strategic partners, not vendors.
- **Startups:** the shortest paths to scale are tolling or joint-venture talks with an amino-acid maker, frozen-food co-packers that already make chay, and cassava glucose bought as fermentation carbon. Otherwise, bring your own extruder or fermenter.
- **Manufacturers:** food-grade soy flour from the crush sector is the obvious feedstock for Vietnamese textured soy.
- **Policy makers and international bodies:** the clearest missing public good is a shared, food-grade pilot plant with open access: small fermenters, an extruder and a spray dryer. Saigon Hi-Tech Park or the national food research institute are natural hosts ([[ch27-policy-options]]).
