# 30. What we do not know, and who can tell us

**In one paragraph.** This report was built from public sources over ten research rounds, and by the end each new search added little. The remaining gaps are of a different kind: they sit in ministry files, company plants and customs records, not on the open web. Most can be closed cheaply, by a written question to a ministry, a call to a feed mill's technical manager or a customs data request. We list the twenty that matter most, ranked by how much they would change a decision in this report, with the play they unblock ([[ch26-plays]]) and who to ask. The full register holds 446 open questions (`open_questions.csv`) and 352 disagreements between sources, each with the position we took (`disagreements.csv`).

> **Demand questions.** Part III adds 114 demand-side open questions (OQ-156 to OQ-269, [[app-r1-open-questions]]). The ones that most change the demand picture are how many people keep chay days, how much meat is eaten as processed products, protein per canteen meal, the food and feed split of plant-protein imports, and any tested Vietnamese evidence on willingness to pay and names ([[ch25-demand-to-frontier]]).

Source: `open_questions.csv` (fields `priority_rank`, `unblocks`, `cheapest_way_to_close`, `owner_org_to_ask`) {VN-direct\|Medium}.

**Reading.** Items 1 to 3 are regulatory and time-sensitive: each could be settled by a single written reply, and together they decide whether most plays have a clear route. Items 4 to 10 decide the economics of T1 to T4 and need industry calls rather than more desk research. Items 11 and 12 affect how large the future feed-protein market looks.

## 30.2 Where sources disagree

We recorded 352 disagreements between sources and stated the position we took for each ([[app-r2-disagreements]]). The ones that most affect this report's conclusions:

| Topic | The disagreement | Position taken |
|---|---|---|
| Aquafeed volume | Industry 3.9 to 4.8 Mt (2025) against USDA 6.5 Mt | Unresolved; both shown, never averaged ([[ch01-why-vietnam]]) |

## 30.3 Limits of this report

- **Tools.** The general web-search budget ran out part-way through the first wave. After that, research used direct reads of known pages, legal databases, scholarly indexes (Scite and OpenAIRE) and trade data (WITS). Some sources could not be reached; each is logged ([[app-m1-method]]).
- **Language.** Vietnamese-language legal texts and press were read directly. Vietnamese-language journals are only partly indexed, so Vietnamese research output is undercounted ([[ch06-knowledge-talent]]).
- **Field data.** The retail audit is a convenience sample of 186 products in 11 stores in two cities in September 2026 ([[app-s2-retail-audit]]).
- **Cost stacks.** Indicative, not feasibility studies ([[ch09-economics]]).
- **No interviews.** This draft relies on documents. The unknowns above are the agenda for a round of interviews.

## 30.4 How to help close the gaps

Readers who hold any of these answers (ministries, feed mills, starch factories, laboratories, investors) can help. AltProtein Vietnam will record corrections in `open_questions.csv` and `disagreements.csv` and date each change, so the website can show what has been closed since publication.
