# 28. Moves that pay off in any 2050

**In brief.**
- A scenario set is useful only if it changes what people do today. We tested 21 candidate moves against the four 2050 worlds of [[ch23-scenarios-2050]]. The moves come from the policy options, the plays and the futures research.
- Seven are no-regret moves (moves that pay off whatever happens). Most are public goods, and most can start in 2026 to 2028 at modest cost.
- Six are options: cheap to hold now, and valuable in some worlds.
- Two are bets that should wait for named signposts, because they pay off in one world and lose money in others. One is a hedge that limits the damage of the bad worlds.
- The most urgent move is a new-food route in the Food Safety Law and its decree, in 2026 to 2027.
- Five feed moves were retired in version 0.8, so 16 of the 21 moves remain.

> **Method note.** We class a move as **no-regret** if it pays off in all four worlds; an **option** if it is cheap to hold and pays off strongly in at least one; a **bet** if it needs a large commitment and pays off in only one or two; and a **hedge** if its main value is limiting losses in the stressed worlds. This follows the EU Joint Research Centre's stress test, which found 8 of 29 policy challenges common to all four of its scenarios; we treat such items as no-regret [@VIS-35] {general|High}. Each classification is our judgement {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. The four worlds are A Regional workshop, B Comfortable price-taker, C Security build-out and D Squeezed importer. Full table: `robust_moves.csv`.

Lead actors use these short forms: MAE (Ministry of Agriculture and Environment), MOH (Ministry of Health), MOST (Ministry of Science and Technology), MOF (Ministry of Finance), NSO (National Statistics Office) and DFIs (development finance institutions).

## 28.1 Seven no-regret moves

| Move | What it involves | Lead actors | Start by | Why it pays off everywhere |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-01 Publish a national protein balance** | A yearly balance of the protein people eat, by food, source and origin, with a memo line for the imported feed behind domestic meat, eggs, milk and farmed fish | MAE; NSO; MOH (National Institute of Nutrition) | 2028 | The two largest levers in the balance model (soybean-meal inclusion and feed conversion, in the memo line) are unmeasured; every foreign target we tracked has a statistic [@VIS-15; @VIS-13] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-03 New-food route with deadlines** | A new-food procedure with statutory timelines and reliance on reference regulators (accepting their approvals) | MOH; MAE | 2027 to 2028 | Needed to import approved ingredients as well as to make them; the Food Safety Law window is open now [@REG-12] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-04 Shared food-grade pilot capacity** | 10,000 to 30,000 L of open, food-grade pilot fermentation, with sensory and food-safety testing for the products made on it | MOST; Ho Chi Minh City; universities; DFIs | 2030 | Rivals site pilot capacity in designated zones: Thailand's Eastern Economic Corridor of Innovation has two 15,000 L fermenters. We found none open in Vietnam [@RGN-36; @FTB-42; @INF-02; @INF-05] {VN-adjacent\|Medium} |
| **RM-05 Protein-quality laboratory and open price series** | Accredited testing for protein quality, authenticity and contaminants; a public price and cost series for food protein ingredients | MOST; MAE; a university | 2028 | Imports and domestic products need the same checks, and adulteration is a listed wildcard, a low-probability, high-impact event ([[app-f2-drivers-signals]]). None of the Vietnamese laboratory pages we read lists amino-acid digestibility tests [@INF-03; @INF-10; @INF-11; @INF-13; @INF-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-06 Residue-carbon rules and a by-product atlas** | Mapped volumes of cassava pulp, straw, stillage (distillery residue), okara (soy pulp from tofu and soy milk) and brewery streams; collection contracts; food-safety rules for food-grade side streams | MAE; provinces; industry | 2028 | Straw must be fully collected on 1 million ha by 2030; EU feed law bans some waste streams [@NTS-32; @NGF-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-08 Climate-proof siting** | Raised sites and surface water in the Mekong Delta; process designs for cooling water at 40 °C | Provinces; MAE; investors | 2027 | Subsidence of about 1.1 cm a year; plants built after 2035 in coastal Ca Mau (former Ca Mau and Bac Lieu) or Vinh Long (former Vinh Long, Tra Vinh and Ben Tre) face flood risk in their lifetime (our inference, as is the 40 °C design value) [@CLM-13; @CLM-14; @CLM-16] {VN-direct\|Medium} {fx:projection} |
| **RM-09 Process-engineering skills** | Fermentation operators and senior process engineers; industry placements | Ministry of Education and Training; MOST; universities; industry | 2027 | Senior biotechnology talent is the named bottleneck; graduate targets already exist [@ECF-39; @ECF-41] {VN-direct\|Medium} |

{{kn:kn-no-regret-moves}}

**The protein balance (RM-01) has a first version.** We built one from FAO food balance sheets: the protein supply by food, the share of each food that is imported, and a memo line for the imported feed behind domestic animal foods ([[ch01-why-vietnam]], section 1.1; open tables in `protein_supply_by_source.csv`, `self_sufficiency_by_food.csv` and `animal_protein_import_reliance.csv`). It shows that 19% to 23% of the protein supply was imported food in 2023 (our calculation) [@DIE-07] {VN-direct|Medium}. With the feed memo line, 61% to 71% of animal protein rested on imports, directly or through feed (our calculation) [@DIE-07; @MAC-01; @FS-23] {VN-direct|Low}. We found no food balance sheet published by a Vietnamese agency (OQ-462) {VN-direct|Medium}. A national version would replace FAO estimates where they look weak, such as eggs and milk ([[app-r2-disagreements]], DG-174 and DG-175).

**An official hook exists for its headline indicator.** The national food-security resolution to 2030 (Resolution 34/NQ-CP, 2021) sets production volumes but no import, feed or protein indicator [@SEC-01] {VN-direct|High}. MAE's 2026 plan asks to "step by step reduce import dependence by developing domestic raw-material areas" [@SEC-02] {VN-direct|High}. A protein balance would let MAE measure that task. Its headline indicator should be the share of the protein people eat made from domestic inputs, defined by where the inputs come from, not where the processing happens. We found no announced successor to Resolution 34 after 2030; one would be a natural home for the indicator (our judgement; OQ-455) {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}.

**The public goods fold in.** Four public goods remain among the plays in [[ch26-plays]]. P1 (a proportionate new-food procedure) becomes RM-03. P4 (a protein-quality laboratory) and P6 (an open cost model and price series) become RM-05. P5 (a by-product atlas) becomes RM-06. In [[ch23-scenarios-2050]], P4 and P6 already scored no-regret, and P1 and P5 held in every world. The related policy options are in [[ch27-policy-options]].

## 28.2 Six options: cheap to hold

| Move | What it involves | Pays off most in | Start by | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-10 Reserve hub sites** | Name food-fermentation hubs (clusters of plants on one site) in the MAE biotechnology-cluster plan (due December 2026) and in provincial hi-tech agriculture zones | A, C (D partly) | 2026 to 2027 | The bioeconomy map is still blank; provinces can now create hi-tech agriculture zones themselves [@HUB-09; @HUB-26; @NTS-37] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-11 Small permanent protein-innovation fund** | USD 5 to 15 M a year for food-protein innovation, reviewed each five-year plan | A, C; useful in all | 2027 | Denmark about USD 12 M, Germany about EUR 6 M (its protein-crop strategy) and Canada about USD 22 M (a plant-protein supercluster) a year; we annualised Denmark and Canada from multi-year totals [@VIS-01; @VIS-24; @VIS-06] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-12 Concessional (below-market) finance window** | DFI co-finance and guarantees with an offtake (an agreement to buy the output) from a named food manufacturer, ready to open when a signpost trips | C, D | 2028 (ready) | Cutting the capital charge from 12% to 9% does as much as the cost learning expected by 2030 (our calculation) {VN-direct\|Low} {fx:estimate} |

| **RM-15 Clear rules for second-hand fermentation equipment** | Customs and tax treatment; a register of available used tanks, 2027 to 2030 | C (A and D partly) | 2027 | Meati's assets sold for USD 75,000 against an estimated USD 15 to 20 M original spend [@FTB-38] {general\|Medium} |

## 28.3 Two bets, each with a trigger

Each bet pays off in one world and loses money in others, so each waits for a trigger. One is contract precision fermentation (microbes engineered to make one protein). The other is sugar from rice straw.

| Bet | Pays off in | Trigger before scaling | Why wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-18 Contract precision-fermentation plant for export** | A (C partly) | A new-food route, an ASEAN reliance mechanism or regional capacity outside China, and an anchor customer | Works today only for high-value proteins [@FTB-41] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-19 Cellulosic sugar from rice straw** | C (A partly) | An Asian straw plant above 80% of design capacity for a full year (our threshold) | Every US and European commercial plant has shut (Brazil's Raizen plant still runs); India's reached 62% [@NGF-11; @NGF-33] {general\|Medium} |

Triggers map to the signposts in `signposts_2050.csv`. A bet placed before its trigger trips is a speculative investment, not a policy.

## 28.4 One hedge

**RM-21 Import monitoring and equal standards for protein imports.** Track the volumes and unit values of plant, microbial and fermented protein-ingredient imports for food. Apply the same food-safety and quality rules to imports and to domestic products. No baseline series exists today {VN-direct|Low}. This limits the damage of a dumping episode (imports sold below cost) in worlds B and D without closing the market.

**Five feed moves retired.** We retired five moves in version 0.8, because feed is now context only. They were RM-02, a feed-efficiency and formulation programme; RM-07, an omega-3 sourcing plan for aquafeed; RM-16, bulk microbial feed-protein capacity; RM-17, a power-to-protein demonstration on the Mekong shrimp coast; and RM-20, diversified feed-protein sourcing and stocks. They stay in `robust_moves.csv` as the record. In the balance model, better feed conversion and formulation would cut 2050 soybean-meal need by about 3.4 Mt. That is about twice what the study's large alternative-protein scenario (S-ALT) would cut (our calculation) {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. That lever sits outside the scope of this study ([[ch22-protein-balance-2050]]).

## 28.5 Sequencing: the first three years

> **For policy makers.** Six of the seven no-regret moves, and one cheap option, can start between September 2026 and the end of 2028. Existing institutions and modest budgets are enough. In order of urgency:
> 1. a new-food route in the Food Safety Law and its decree (RM-03, 2026 to 2027);
> 2. named sites in the MAE biotechnology-cluster plan (RM-10, an option, December 2026);
> 3. climate-proof siting rules for new plants (RM-08, 2027);
> 4. a skills programme linked to Decision 1002 on the high-tech workforce (RM-09, 2027);
> 5. a first national protein balance (RM-01, 2028);
> 6. a by-product atlas and residue rules (RM-06, 2028);
> 7. a protein-quality laboratory and open price series (RM-05, 2028).
>
> Shared food-grade pilot capacity (RM-04) follows by 2030.

## 28.7 What we could not resolve

Several open questions decide which world is unfolding and how large the moves should be. The most important are:
- whether any Vietnamese agency compiles a food or protein balance sheet, the base for RM-01 (OQ-462);
- Vietnamese time series of feed conversion and soybean-meal inclusion, which the feed memo line of the protein balance needs;
- the purity and volume of carbon dioxide (CO2) at named sites;
- whether full-scale fermentation plants qualify for the incentives of hi-tech agriculture zones;
- whether Vietnam's next climate plan under the Paris Agreement (NDC 3.0) will add agriculture or livestock caps for 2035.

All are logged in [[app-r1-open-questions]] with the futures prefix, and disagreements between sources in [[app-r2-disagreements]].
