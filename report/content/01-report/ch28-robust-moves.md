# 28. Moves that pay off in any 2050

**In brief.**
- A scenario set is useful only if it changes what people do today. We tested 16 moves against the four 2050 worlds of [[ch23-scenarios-2050]].
- Seven are **no-regret moves** that pay off whatever happens. Most are public goods, and most can start in 2026 to 2028 at modest cost.
- Six are **options**: cheap to hold now, valuable in some worlds.
- Two are **bets** that should wait for named signposts. One is a **hedge** against the bad worlds.
- The most urgent move is a new-food route in the Food Safety Law and its decree, in 2026 to 2027.

> **Method note.** A move is **no-regret** if it pays off in all four worlds; an **option** if it is cheap to hold and pays off strongly in at least one; a **bet** if it needs a large commitment and pays off in only one or two; and a **hedge** if its main value is limiting losses in the stressed worlds [@VIS-35] {general|High}. Each class is our judgement {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. The four worlds are A Regional workshop, B Comfortable price-taker, C Security build-out and D Squeezed importer. Play codes such as T5 refer to [[ch26-plays]].

## 28.1 Seven no-regret moves

| Move | What it involves | Lead actors | Start by | Why it pays off everywhere |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-01 Publish a national protein balance** | A yearly balance of the protein people eat, by food, source and origin, with the imported feed behind it | Agriculture ministry; statistics office; National Institute of Nutrition | 2028 | Every foreign target we tracked rests on a statistic [@VIS-15; @VIS-13] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-03 New-food route with deadlines** | A new-food procedure with deadlines and reliance on trusted regulators, including a safety lane for cultivated food | Health ministry; agriculture ministry | 2027 to 2028 | Needed to import approved ingredients as well as make them; the law window is open now [@REG-12; @SLC-05; @SLC-06; @RGN-04] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-04 Shared food-grade pilot capacity** | 10,000 to 30,000 L of open, food-grade pilot fermentation, with taste and safety testing | Science ministry; Ho Chi Minh City; universities; development banks | 2030 | Rivals have it (Thailand has two 15,000 L tanks); we found none open in Vietnam [@RGN-36; @FTB-42; @INF-02; @INF-05] {VN-adjacent\|Medium} |
| **RM-05 Protein-quality laboratory and open price series** | Accredited protein-quality and safety testing; a public price series for protein ingredients, including domestic bean prices | Science ministry; agriculture ministry; a university | 2028 | Imports and domestic products need the same checks; no Vietnamese laboratory offers digestibility tests [@INF-03; @INF-10; @INF-11; @INF-13; @INF-14; @PPE-05] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-06 Residue-carbon rules and a by-product atlas** | Mapped volumes of cassava pulp, straw, okara and brewery streams; collection contracts; food-safety rules for food-grade side streams | Agriculture ministry; provinces; industry | 2028 | Rice straw must be fully collected on 1 million ha by 2030 [@NTS-32; @NGF-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-08 Climate-proof siting** | Raised sites and surface water in the Mekong Delta; plants designed for warm cooling water | Provinces; investors | 2027 | The delta sinks about 1.1 cm a year, so coastal plants built after 2035 face flood risk in their lifetime (our inference) [@CLM-13; @CLM-14; @CLM-16] {VN-direct\|Medium} {fx:projection} |
| **RM-09 Process-engineering skills** | Two tracks: a master's for senior engineers, and operator training through industry ministry colleges and the national food research institute; plus a product challenge lab | Education, industry and science ministries; universities; industry | 2027 | Senior biotech talent is the named bottleneck; no alternative-protein or industrial fermentation course exists [@ECF-39; @ECF-41; @VCO-21; @SLW-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |

{{kn:kn-no-regret-moves}}

**The protein balance (RM-01) has a first version.** We built one from FAO food balance sheets ([[ch01-why-vietnam]], section 1.1). It shows that 19% to 23% of the protein supply was imported food in 2023. Counting feed, 61% to 71% of animal protein rested on imports (our calculation) [@DIE-07; @MAC-01; @FS-23] {VN-direct|Low}. We found none published by a Vietnamese agency {VN-direct|Medium}. A national version would also fix weak FAO figures, such as eggs and milk. Its method matches Europe's [@PPE-03; @PPE-04] {general|High}.

**It has an official hook.** The food-security resolution to 2030 has no import or protein indicator [@SEC-01] {VN-direct|High}. But the agriculture ministry's 2026 plan asks to "step by step reduce import dependence" [@SEC-02] {VN-direct|High}. A protein balance would let it measure that. The headline indicator should be the share of protein eaten that is made from domestic inputs, by where the inputs come from, not where processing happens (our judgement) {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}.

**Skills (RM-09) has two tracks and a product lab.**
- **Senior engineers.** A 2025 national programme funds 100 talent master's programmes, and universities are asked to pair biotechnology with application fields [@SLW-07; @SLW-08] {VN-direct|Medium}. A "biotechnology plus food process engineering" master's fits (our suggestion).
- **Operators.** That programme has no operator track [@SLW-07] {VN-direct|Medium}. The industry ministry runs the food colleges and the national food research institute, which has a fermentation department [@SLW-17; @INF-01] {VN-direct|Medium}. So it should lead.
- **The constraint is equipment, not fees.** A hands-on US fermentation course costs about USD 3,100; a first cohort of 20 would cost about USD 62,000 in fees (our calculation) [@SLW-09] {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. But shared pilot capacity comes only by 2030, so early training must use industrial tanks or go abroad.
- **A teaching capstone** on the shared line, once it exists, would also give it steady users [@AML-32] {general|High}.
- **A product challenge lab:** a one-semester course where student teams solve problems set and part-funded by Vietnamese buyers. Berkeley ran such classes for seven years, producing at least three startups [@AML-21; @AML-22; @AML-11; @AML-13; @AML-15; @AML-33] {general|Medium}. Drop it if no firm sets a problem in the first cycle.

**The public goods fold in.** The new-food procedure (P1) becomes RM-03. The protein-quality laboratory (P4) and price series (P6) become RM-05. The by-product atlas (P5) becomes RM-06 ([[ch27-policy-options]]).

## 28.2 Six options: cheap to hold

| Move | What it involves | Pays off most in | Start by | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-10 Reserve hub sites** | Name food-fermentation hubs in the national biotechnology cluster plan (due December 2026) and in provincial high-tech farm zones | A, C | 2026 to 2027 | The map is still blank; provinces can now create such zones [@HUB-09; @HUB-26; @NTS-37] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-11 Small permanent protein-innovation fund** | USD 5 to 15 M a year for food-protein innovation, reviewed each five-year plan | A, C; useful in all | 2027 | Denmark, Germany and Canada spend similar sums a year [@VIS-01; @VIS-24; @VIS-06] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-12 Cheap-finance window** | Development-bank co-finance and guarantees for first fermentation plants (T4, T5 scale), with a buyer's agreement to take the output; ready when a signpost trips | C, D | 2028 | Cutting the cost of capital from 12% to 9% does as much as the cost learning expected by 2030 (our calculation) {VN-direct\|Low} {fx:estimate} |
| **RM-13 Join regional approval and statistics networks** | Take part in Southeast Asian reliance work and the EU-style protein balance method | A, C | 2027 | Reliance is how smaller regulators keep pace {general\|Low}; the EU is building such balances [@VIS-15] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-14 Seed research on cultivated seafood and gas fermentation** | Small programmes with foreign partners on Vietnamese species; open flavour profiles of farmed fish | A, C | 2028 | Vietnam has 2 of 494 gas-fermentation papers [@FTG-14] {VN-direct\|High}; cell lines exist abroad for grouper and seabass [@SLS-14; @SLS-15] {general\|Low} |
| **RM-15 Clear rules for second-hand fermentation equipment** | Customs and tax treatment; a register of used tanks, 2027 to 2030 | C | 2027 | One US firm's tanks sold for a fraction of their cost [@FTB-38; @SLF-12; @SLF-14] {general\|Medium} |

**The innovation fund (RM-11) is large by Vietnamese standards:** half to one and a half times everything the national science foundation approved for new projects in 2023 (our calculation) [@SLW-01] {VN-direct|Medium}. A first step would be one themed research call of about USD 0.35 million ([[ch06-knowledge-talent]]) {VN-direct|Medium} {fx:estimate}.

**The cheap-finance window (RM-12) is for fermentation plants, not textured soy.** Existing loan guarantees and leases already cover a textured-protein line [@SLK-20; @SLK-01; @SLK-10; @SLK-21] {VN-direct|High}. The window matters at the scale of tens of millions of USD.

**Seed research (RM-14) adds species and flavour.** Asian seabass and grouper are farmed in Vietnam, and food-oriented cell lines exist abroad; catfish can serve as a cheap model species [@SLS-13; @SLS-14; @SLS-15; @SLS-16; @SCI-10] {general|Low}. An open flavour dataset for cooked catfish, tilapia and carp would serve makers of plant-based, blended and cultivated seafood. One research funder has asked for exactly such data [@CGG-03; @CAP-57; @CGG-15; @CGG-16] {general|Medium}.

## 28.3 Two bets, each with a trigger

Each bet pays off in one world and loses money in others, so each waits for a trigger.

| Bet | Pays off in | Trigger before scaling | Why wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-18 Contract precision-fermentation plant for export** | A | A new-food route, a regional approval system or capacity outside China, and an anchor customer | Works today only for high-value proteins [@FTB-41] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-19 Sugar from rice straw** | C | An Asian straw plant above 80% of capacity for a full year (our threshold) | Every Western commercial plant has shut; India's reached 62% [@NGF-11; @NGF-33] {general\|Medium} |

A bet placed before its trigger trips is speculation, not policy.

## 28.4 One hedge

**RM-21 Import monitoring and equal standards.** Track the volumes and prices of protein-ingredient imports for food, including soybeans, and apply the same safety rules to imports and domestic products. No baseline series exists today {VN-direct|Low}. This limits the damage of dumping in worlds B and D without closing the market.

Soybean imports are concentrated: 89% came from Brazil and the US in 2025 (our calculation) [@MAC-09] {VN-direct|High}. When the US briefly embargoed soybean exports in 1973, Japan responded by financing a new supply origin in Brazil [@PPE-12] {general|Medium}.

**Feed moves are retired,** because feed is context only. Better feed efficiency would cut 2050 soybean meal need about twice as much as the alternative-protein scenario, but sits outside this study ([[ch22-protein-balance-2050]]) {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}.

## 28.5 Sequencing: the first three years

> **For policy makers.** Six no-regret moves and one cheap option can start between now and the end of 2028, with existing institutions and modest budgets. In order of urgency:
> 1. a new-food route in the Food Safety Law and its decree (RM-03, 2026 to 2027);
> 2. named sites in the national biotechnology cluster plan (RM-10, December 2026);
> 3. climate-proof siting rules (RM-08, 2027);
> 4. two skills tracks (RM-09, 2027);
> 5. a first national protein balance (RM-01, 2028);
> 6. a by-product atlas and residue rules (RM-06, 2028);
> 7. a protein-quality laboratory and price series (RM-05, 2028).
>
> Shared food-grade pilot capacity (RM-04) follows by 2030.

## 28.6 By actor: the first move

| Actor | First move | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| Agriculture ministry | Commission the national protein balance | The statistic underpins every target |
| Health ministry | A new-food procedure with deadlines and reliance | The one window that closes |
| Science ministry and Ho Chi Minh City | An open food-grade pilot plant | Rivals already have one |
| Finance ministry and development banks | A ready cheap-finance window for fermentation plants | Cheaper capital does as much as the learning expected by 2030 |
| Education and industry ministries, universities | The two skills tracks | Skills take years, and the pilot line will need staff |
| Delta and cassava provinces | Raised sites with surface water; residue-collection contracts | Land and water decide 25-year plant economics |
| Feed mills | Share anonymised feed data | The protein balance needs it |
| Investors | Back plays that hold in every world; hold bets with named triggers | Returns depend on which world arrives |
| Startups | Koji and fungal foods (T9), or mung bean and rice protein for drinks (T10) | Both hold in every world |
| Researchers | Process know-how on local strains; the measurements behind the protein balance | Fills the largest evidence gaps |
| International bodies | Technical help on the protein balance and the review cycle | A public good others can use |

## 28.7 What we could not resolve

The questions that most decide which world is unfolding, and how big the moves should be:
- whether any Vietnamese agency compiles a protein balance;
- Vietnamese data on feed conversion and soybean meal use;
- the purity and volume of carbon dioxide at named sites;
- whether fermentation plants qualify for high-tech farm-zone incentives;
- whether Vietnam's next climate plan will cap farm or livestock emissions for 2035;
- which plants train fermentation operators, and whether any would take outside trainees.
