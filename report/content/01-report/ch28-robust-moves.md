# 28. Robust moves: what to do now that pays off in any 2050

**In one paragraph.** A scenario set is useful only if it changes what people do today. We tested 21 candidate moves, drawn from the policy options and plays and from the futures research, against the four worlds of [[ch23-scenarios-2050]]. Nine are no-regret: they pay off whichever world arrives, most are public goods, and most can start in 2026 to 2028 at modest cost. Six are options: cheap to hold now, valuable in some worlds. Four are bets that should wait for named signposts, because they pay off in one world and lose money in others. Two are hedges that limit the damage of the bad worlds. The single strongest lever in the evidence, feed efficiency and formulation, is not an alternative-protein technology at all; it is the base on which every alternative protein will be sold.

> **Method note.** A move is **no-regret** if it pays off in all four worlds; an **option** if it is cheap to hold and pays off strongly in at least one; a **bet** if it needs a large commitment and pays off in only one or two; a **hedge** if its main value is limiting losses in the stressed worlds. This follows the EU Joint Research Centre's stress test, which found 8 of 29 policy challenges common to all four of its scenarios; we treat such items as no-regret [@VIS-35] {general|High}. Each classification is our judgement {VN-direct|Low} {fx:estimate}. Full table: `robust_moves.csv`.

## 28.1 Nine no-regret moves

| Move | What it involves | Lead actors | Start by | Why it pays off everywhere |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-01 Publish a national protein and feed balance** | Yearly balance of feed protein by source and origin, with soybean-meal inclusion and feed conversion by species | MAE; NSO; feed association | 2028 | The two largest levers in the balance model are unmeasured; every tracked foreign target has a statistic [@VIS-15; @VIS-13] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-02 Feed-efficiency and formulation programme** | Amino acids, low-protein diets, precision feeding, by-product meals, feed-conversion benchmarks; soybean-meal inclusion falling 1 to 2% a year (relative) | MAE; integrators; feed mills; universities | 2027 | At 1% a year, with faster feed-conversion gains, cuts 2050 soybean-meal need by about 3.4 Mt in our balance model (QNT-model); China's relative decline since 2017 was about 1 to 3% a year, depending on the data series (our calculation) [@GEO-10; @GEO-11] {general\|Medium} {fx:estimate} |
| **RM-03 New-food and new-feed-ingredient routes with deadlines** | A new-food procedure with statutory timelines and reliance on reference regulators; aquafeed entries for microbial biomass and algal oils | MOH; MAE | 2027 to 2028 | Needed to import approved ingredients as well as to make them; the Food Safety Law window is open now [@REG-12; @REG2-01] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-04 Shared pilot and feed-trial capacity** | 10,000 to 30,000 L of open, food-grade pilot fermentation; public tank and pond trials | MOST; Ho Chi Minh City; universities; DFIs | 2030 | Rivals site pilot capacity in designated zones (Thailand's EECi has two 15,000 L fermenters); we found none open in Vietnam [@RGN-36; @FTB-42; @INF-02; @INF-05] {VN-adjacent\|Medium} |
| **RM-05 Protein-quality laboratory and open price series** | Accredited testing for protein quality, authenticity and contaminants; a public price and cost series | MOST; MAE; a university | 2028 | Imports and domestic products need the same checks; adulteration is a listed wildcard ([[app-f2-drivers-signals]]); none of the Vietnamese laboratory pages we read lists amino-acid digestibility tests [@INF-03; @INF-10; @INF-11; @INF-13; @INF-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-06 Residue-carbon rules and a by-product atlas** | Mapped volumes of cassava pulp, straw, stillage and brewery streams; collection contracts; feed-safety rules for side streams that match export-market rules | MAE; provinces; industry | 2028 | Straw must be fully collected on 1 million ha by 2030; EU feed law bans some waste streams [@NTS-32; @NGF-14] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-07 Omega-3 sourcing plan for aquafeed** | Qualify algal and oilseed omega-3 oils in shrimp and marine-fish feeds; track EPA plus DHA needs | Feed mills; MAE; research institutes | 2028 | Omega-3 is a tighter constraint than protein (our judgement), and the alternatives are commercial [@AQF-16; @AQF-19; @AQF-23] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-08 Climate-proof siting** | Raised sites and surface water in the Mekong; process designs for cooling water at 40 °C | Provinces; MAE; investors | 2027 | Subsidence of about 1.1 cm a year; plants built after 2035 in coastal Ca Mau or Vinh Long face flood risk in their lifetime (our inference, as is the 40 °C design value) [@CLM-13; @CLM-14; @CLM-16] {VN-direct\|Medium} {fx:projection} |
| **RM-09 Process-engineering skills** | Fermentation operators and senior process engineers; industry placements | MOET; MOST; universities; industry | 2027 | Senior biotechnology talent is the named bottleneck; graduate targets already exist [@ECF-39; @ECF-41] {VN-direct\|Medium} |

## 28.2 Six options: cheap to hold

| Move | What it involves | Pays off most in | Start by | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-10 Reserve hub sites** | Name fermentation and feed-protein sites in the MAE biotechnology-cluster plan (due December 2026) and in provincial hi-tech agriculture zones | A, C (D partly) | 2026 to 2027 | The bioeconomy map is still blank; provinces can now create hi-tech agriculture zones themselves [@HUB-09; @HUB-26; @NTS-37] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-11 Small permanent protein-innovation fund** | USD 5 to 15 M a year, reviewed each five-year plan | A, C; useful in all | 2027 | Denmark about USD 12 M, Germany about EUR 6 M (its protein-crop strategy) and Canada about USD 22 M (a plant-protein supercluster) a year; Denmark and Canada annualised by us from multi-year totals [@VIS-01; @VIS-24; @VIS-06] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-12 Concessional finance window** | DFI co-finance and guarantees with a feed-mill offtake, ready to open when a signpost trips | C, D | 2028 (ready) | Cutting the capital charge from 12% to 9% does as much as the cost learning expected by 2030 (our calculation) {VN-direct\|Low} {fx:estimate} |
| **RM-13 Join regional approval and statistics networks** | Take part in any ASEAN reliance work and in the EU-style balance-sheet method | A, C | 2027 | Reliance is how smaller regulators keep pace (our judgement) {general\|Low}; the EU is developing harmonised feed and food protein balance sheets [@VIS-15] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-14 Seed research on cultivated seafood cells and gas fermentation** | Small programmes with foreign partners on Vietnamese species and gas-fermentation strains | A, C | 2028 | Vietnam has 2 of 494 gas-fermentation papers (OpenAlex, September 2026) [@FTG-14] {VN-direct\|High} |
| **RM-15 Clear rules for second-hand fermentation equipment** | Customs and tax treatment; a register of available used tanks, 2027 to 2030 | C (A and D partly) | 2027 | Meati's assets sold for USD 75,000 against an estimated USD 15 to 20 M original spend [@FTB-38] {general\|Medium} |

## 28.3 Four bets, each with a trigger

| Bet | Pays off in | Trigger before scaling | Why wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RM-16 Bulk microbial feed-protein capacity beyond about 1% of feed protein** | C (D partly) | Fishmeal above USD 2,500 per t for 12 months, and pilot cost within 1.2 times fishmeal-protein parity | The highest share we found is 0.4% novel ingredients in Norwegian salmon feed (2020) [@VIS-18] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-17 Protein-from-power demonstration on the Mekong shrimp coast** | C (A partly) | Delivered green hydrogen below USD 2 per kg and firm renewable power below USD 45 per MWh | The five conditions of [[ch21-frontier-technology]]; the flagship hydrogen plant had only a fence, gate and part of its roads in May 2026 [@HUB-05] {VN-direct\|Medium} |
| **RM-18 Contract precision-fermentation plant for export** | A (C partly) | A new-food route, an ASEAN reliance mechanism or regional capacity outside China, and an anchor customer | Works today only for high-value proteins [@FTB-41] {general\|Medium} |
| **RM-19 Cellulosic sugar from rice straw** | C (A partly) | An Asian straw plant above 80% of design capacity for a full year (our threshold) | Every US and European commercial plant has shut (Brazil's Raizen plant still runs); India's reached 62% [@NGF-11; @NGF-33] {general\|Medium} |

Triggers map to the signposts in `signposts_2050.csv`. A bet placed before its trigger trips is a speculative investment, not a policy.

## 28.4 Two hedges

- **RM-20 Diversified feed-protein sourcing and working stocks.** Multi-origin contracts, formulation flexibility and stocks of soybean meal and fishmeal sized to recent shocks. The 2026 Middle East conflict helped raise Vietnamese feed prices 10 to 14% between January and April 2026 (poultry association estimate) [@MAC-24; @COST-33] {VN-direct|Medium}. Protects against worlds C and D; costs little in A and B.
- **RM-21 Import monitoring and equal standards for protein imports.** Track volumes and unit values of microbial and fermented protein imports, and apply the same feed-safety and quality rules to imports and domestic products. No baseline series exists today {VN-direct|Low}. Limits the damage of a dumping episode in worlds B and D without closing the market.

## 28.5 Sequencing: the first three years

> **For policy makers.** Between September 2026 and the end of 2028, eight of the nine no-regret moves can start with existing institutions and modest budgets, alongside one cheap option. In order of urgency: (1) a new-food route in the Food Safety Law and its decree (RM-03, 2026 to 2027); (2) named sites in the MAE biotechnology-cluster plan (RM-10, an option, December 2026); (3) a feed-efficiency and formulation programme under the existing feed scheme (RM-02, 2027); (4) climate-proof siting rules for new plants (RM-08, 2027); (5) a skills programme linked to Decision 1002 (RM-09, 2027); (6) a first protein and feed balance (RM-01, 2028); (7) a by-product atlas and residue rules (RM-06, 2028); (8) a protein-quality laboratory and open price series (RM-05, 2028); (9) an omega-3 sourcing plan (RM-07, 2028). Shared pilot capacity (RM-04) follows by 2030.

## 28.6 By actor: the first move

| Actor | First move | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| MAE | Commission the protein and feed balance; add microbial biomass and algal oils to the aquafeed lists | The statistic enables every target; the lists are the cheapest route opening |
| MOH | A new-food procedure with timelines and reliance | The one window that closes (2026 to 2027) |
| MOST and Ho Chi Minh City | An open food-grade pilot plant | Rivals already have one |
| MOF and DFIs | A ready-to-open concessional window | Cheaper capital does as much as the learning expected by 2030 |
| Provinces (Tay Ninh, Ca Mau, Vinh Long, Dong Thap, Can Tho) | Raised, surface-water sites and residue-collection contracts | Land and water decide 25-year plant economics |
| Feed mills and integrators | Share anonymised inclusion and conversion data; qualify one non-marine omega-3 oil | The largest lever is theirs |
| Investors | Underwrite robust plays; hold bets as options with named triggers | Returns depend on which world arrives |
| Startups | Species-specific functional feed ingredients on local carbon | Holds in every world |
| Research bodies | Measurement of feed conversion and inclusion; process know-how on local strains | Fills the two largest gaps in the evidence |
| International bodies | Technical help on the balance sheet and the review cycle | A public good others can use |

## 28.7 What we could not resolve

The futures round leaves open questions that decide which world is unfolding and how large the moves should be. The most important: the current status of Thai Duong Feed's yeast-protein line; Vietnamese time series of feed conversion and soybean-meal inclusion; the purity and volume of CO2 at named sites; whether full-scale fermentation plants qualify for hi-tech agriculture zone incentives; and whether NDC 3.0 will add agriculture or livestock caps for 2035. All are logged in [[app-r1-open-questions]] with the futures prefix, and disagreements between sources in [[app-r2-disagreements]].
