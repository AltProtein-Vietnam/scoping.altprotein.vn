| Demand route | Who buys | What it must be | Displaces meat? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic textured protein replacing imports | Chay makers, co-packers, distributors, caterers | At Chinese landed cost (duty-free, about USD 1.29 to 1.38 per kg); flour price decides | No |
| Higher-protein chay foods | Households through incumbent brands and retailers' own labels | Today's chay shelf price; 10 g protein per 100 g for a high-protein claim | No (chay days are already meat-free) |
| Hybrid processed meat | Meat processors | Cheaper than hydrated soy, which already beats lean meat; declared as an ingredient, at 10 to 20% of the meat in fine emulsions and up to 30% in coarse mince | Yes |
| Canteen and school protein | Contract caterers; school kitchens | Beat dry soy pieces and tofu per gram of protein; in schools only a labelled blend removes meat | Yes, at the meal (not in the school plant slot) |
| Plant-milk protein | Vinamilk, Vinasoy, Nutifood | Soy-free, UHT-stable isolate; drinks at or below 5 g sugar per 100 ml | No |
| Export | European importers of Asian wrapped foods; diaspora | Duty-free lines; IFS or BRCGS; origin-compliant wrappers | No (outside Vietnam) |

| Horizon | Businesses (plays) | Public steps |
|---|---|---|
| Now to 2028 | T1 domestic textured plant protein; T2 functional microbial feed ingredients; T4 cassava-starch-to-sugar hub (if a fermenter commits); pilots of koji (T9) and duckweed feed (T6) | New-food clause in the Food Safety Law (first comments October 2026; vote considered May 2027); note on precision-fermented proteins; aquafeed and livestock feed-list fixes |
| 2028 to 2031 | T5 shared pilot and tolling fermentation; T10 mung bean and rice protein; T7 contract-made precision fermentation; T3 bulk fishmeal replacement if costs fall | Novel-food decree with a reliance route for foods approved by trusted regulators; public feed trials; protein-quality laboratory; capital grants over tax holidays |
