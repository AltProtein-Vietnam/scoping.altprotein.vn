## Where the protein people eat comes from ([[ch01-why-vietnam]], [[ch11-protein-diet]])

About one fifth of the protein supply is imported food. Most of the meat, eggs and farmed fish Vietnamese people eat is produced in Vietnam, but on imported feed. Counting that feed, about two thirds of the animal protein people eat rests on imports (our calculation).

## Food security: disease, wild fish and what new protein would change ([[ch01-why-vietnam]], [[ch20-drivers-2050]])

Disease, not feed, is the protein shock consumers feel. Wild fish is the protein food under most pressure. We found no evidence that alternative protein lowers disease or pandemic risk. Only protein made from domestic inputs reduces import dependence, and at the scale demand supports, the effect on national exposure is small (our calculation).

## Futures: 2035 and 2050 ([[ch19-outlook-2035]] to [[ch24-vision-2050]])

Every value below is a published projection, our estimate or, for the vision, a normative goal. None is a forecast.

On trend, the meat protein Vietnamese people eat rises from about 1.01 Mt in 2025 to about 1.45 Mt in 2050 (our model). The share of it resting on imports, directly or through feed, rises from 70% to 78% to about 79% to 87%. The imported soybean meal behind it rises from 7.2 Mt to about 10.4 Mt. Replacing 1 t of meat protein in diets avoids about 4.5 t of soybean-meal and 9.5 t of maize imports (our calculation). A plant food made from imported soy still keeps the dependence.

Seven no-regret moves pay off in all four worlds, starting with a national protein balance, a new-food route with deadlines and shared food-grade pilot capacity ([[ch28-robust-moves]]).

**The vision for 2050 is a goal, not a forecast** ([[ch24-vision-2050]]). New routes would provide about 67,000 to 172,000 t of protein a year on the benchmark and stretch paths of the demand model. About 59,000 to 147,000 t of it would be made in Vietnam. Meat stays the main protein; new protein adds a second source. For security, what counts is the share made from domestic inputs, not the share made in Vietnam.

## What to do ([[ch25-demand-to-frontier]] to [[ch30-unknowns]])

Seven plays; three lead under almost every weighting: domestic textured plant protein (T1), a cassava-starch-to-fermentation-sugar hub (T4, a cluster of plants on one site) and shared pilot and tolling fermentation (T5).

| Horizon | Businesses (plays) | Public steps |
|---|---|---|
| Now to 2028 | T1 domestic textured plant protein; T4 cassava-starch-to-sugar hub (if a fermenter commits); pilots of koji and fungal foods (T9) | New-food clause in the Food Safety Law (first reading October 2026; vote considered May 2027); note on precision-fermented proteins |
| 2028 to 2031 | T5 shared pilot and tolling fermentation; T10 mung bean and rice protein; T7 contract-made precision fermentation | Novel-food procedure in the implementing decree, with a reliance route for foods approved by trusted regulators; shared food-grade pilot line; protein-quality laboratory; capital grants over tax holidays |
| 2031 onward | T8 cultivated seafood if a food cell line exists | Regional reliance on novel-food approvals |
