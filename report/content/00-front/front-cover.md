# Alternative protein in Vietnam

## A supply-side scoping study

**AltProtein Vietnam · Version 1.0 · November 2026**

**Protein for people first; feed only as context.** Vietnamese people eat more meat each year. Most of it is raised in Vietnam on imported feed. Counting that feed, about two thirds of the animal protein people eat rests on imports. So the case for making protein at home is first a security case: real, but narrow.

This study asks what Vietnam could make at home for people to eat. It covers textured plant protein, fermentation, and cultivated meat and seafood: from which raw materials, with which equipment, under which rules, at what cost, and with whose money and skills. It also asks who would buy new protein, looks ahead to 2035 and 2050, and ends with what each type of actor could do.

Every claim carries a tag for the kind of evidence behind it and a confidence level. Every number links to its source. Claims about the future also say whether they are a trend, projection, estimate, signal, wildcard or vision. Claims about demand say whether the evidence is stated, revealed, tested or inferred.

The study draws on 2,669 sources, 91 companies, 83 facilities and 186 audited retail products. Its protein balance and demand models are reproducible.

*Scoping research, not investment, legal or engineering advice. Scope: the supply side, protein for people first, feed only as context, and demand as a design input.*
